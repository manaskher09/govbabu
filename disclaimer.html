The most important thing to know about GovBabu, in one place.

Not a government website

GovBabu is an independent, privately-run platform built to help aspirants navigate government exams more easily. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by UPSC, SSC, IBPS, Railway (RRB), BPSC, any state PSC, or any other government body or ministry.

Information accuracy

Exam details on GovBabu — seats, eligibility, fees, dates, vacancies and how-to-apply steps — are researched against each exam's official notification wherever one is reachable, and we flag it plainly when something couldn't be independently confirmed. Application windows, dates and other details can and do change on the official side, sometimes with little notice. Always cross-check against the official notification (linked on every exam's page) before you rely on any detail here, especially before submitting an application or payment.

No guarantee of outcomes

Using GovBabu does not guarantee eligibility, selection, or any particular exam outcome. Final eligibility and selection are decided solely by the conducting body under its own rules.

External links

GovBabu links out to official notifications, application portals and result pages. We aren't responsible for the content, availability or accuracy of those external, third-party sites.

Document tools

Photo/signature resizing and PDF tools are provided to help you meet a published specification, but you're responsible for confirming the final files meet the current official requirement before you submit them.

Questions

If something here looks wrong or out of date, please tell us via the Contact page — data corrections are treated as high priority.
