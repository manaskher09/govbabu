FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is GovBabu?

GovBabu helps Indian government-exam aspirants discover exams, understand eligibility and notification details, prepare their application documents, and stay updated — all in one place.

Is GovBabu a government website?

No. GovBabu is an independent platform and is not affiliated with UPSC, SSC, IBPS, Railway, BPSC or any government body.

Is GovBabu free?

Yes — GovBabu is free to use for every aspirant right now, with no login required.

Where does exam information come from?

Exam details are researched and sourced from official notifications where available. Always confirm against the current official notification before submitting your application.

Can I use GovBabu without creating an account?

Yes — nothing on GovBabu currently requires an account or login.

Are my photos and signatures uploaded to a server?

No — photo and signature processing happens directly in your browser and is never uploaded to our servers.

Can GovBabu tell me which exams I am eligible for?

Not yet — personalized eligibility matching is planned and will be clearly labeled "Coming soon" when it's available. Today, eligibility details are shown per exam for you to check yourself.
