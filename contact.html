Frequently asked questions

Is GovBabu free to use?

Yes — everything on GovBabu is free right now for every aspirant, no login and no payment. This is guaranteed for at least the first two months after launch. A small one-time fee may return later for the document-prep tools only; exam information will always stay free.

Is GovBabu an official government website?

No. GovBabu is an independent, privately-run tool and is not affiliated with UPSC, SSC, IBPS, Railway, BPSC or any government body. We research and link to each exam's official notification so you can always verify details on the source yourself.

Is my photo, signature or personal data uploaded anywhere?

Your files are processed directly in your browser — photo and signature resizing, PDF conversion and compression all happen on your own device — and are not uploaded to GovBabu's servers for processing.

How accurate is the exam information?

Every seat count, eligibility rule, pay level and deadline is researched against the official notification wherever one is available, with sources cited. Where something can't be confirmed or sources disagree, we say so instead of guessing — but application windows and dates change often, so always cross-check the official notification linked on each exam's page before you submit anything.

My exam isn't listed — can you add it?

Most likely, yes. We're actively expanding coverage. Message us on Telegram to tell us which exam you need, and we'll research and add it.

I found an error in the exam details — what do I do?

Please report it on Telegram with as much detail as you can (exam name, the incorrect field, and a source if you have one). We treat data corrections as high priority.

Do I need to create an account to use GovBabu?

No. There's no sign-up or login required for any part of the site.

Where does GovBabu get its exam information from?

Primarily from each exam's official notification — the PDF or page published by the conducting body (UPSC, SSC, IBPS, Railway, a state PSC, etc.). Where a detail can't be confirmed against a primary source, we say so in the exam's data note instead of guessing, and cite secondary sources when they're used.

How often is exam information updated?

Whenever we research or re-verify an exam, not on a fixed daily schedule. Application windows, dates and vacancy counts change often on the official side, so always cross-check the linked official notification before you submit anything.

Which exams does GovBabu cover?

SSC, Railway (RRB), Banking (IBPS/SBI/RBI), UPSC, State PSCs, Defence (NDA/CDS/Agniveer), Teaching and Police exams — see the full, current list on Browse Exams. We're actively adding more.
