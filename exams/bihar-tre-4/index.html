📝 How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) at onlinebpsc.bihar.gov.in, including DigiLocker linking, if not already registered
  2. Complete and lock your OTR profile with personal, address, qualification and experience details, and upload photo/signature
  3. Go to the "New Application" section and select the TRE 4.0 (Advt. 14/2026) link
  4. Fill in post/subject/district preferences and TET/STET details
  5. Pay the application fee online — reportedly ₹100 per level applied for, plus ₹200 biometric fee if Aadhaar is not linked (not independently confirmed)
  6. Submit and download the confirmation page

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is Bihar TRE 4.0 a single exam or prelims + mains like TRE 3.0?

Based on available secondary sources, TRE 4.0 is described as a single combined written exam (150 questions/150 marks), similar to the simplified format BPSC used in TRE 3.0, rather than a separate prelims-and-mains structure — but confirm this against the final official notification since details were still being finalized at research time.

Does the subject-specific section differ by the class level I'm applying for?

Yes — Primary (Classes 1-5) teachers are tested mainly on Language and General Studies with no separate subject paper, while Secondary and Higher Secondary applicants (Classes 6-8, 9-10, 11-12) have an additional 80-mark section on their specific teaching subject.

Is there negative marking in TRE 4.0?

This is unclear from available sources — some report 1/3 mark negative marking, others report none, so verify directly against the official BPSC TRE 4.0 notification before assuming either way.

Is the Rs. 100 fee refundable if I don't clear the exam?

No — like previous BPSC exam fees, this is a non-refundable processing fee, not tied to whether you pass or fail.
