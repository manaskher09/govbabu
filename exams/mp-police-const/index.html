📝 How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Register on esb.mp.gov.in using Aadhaar-based e-KYC authentication
  2. Fill personal, educational and category details in the online application form
  3. Generate the combined Photo + Signature + Declaration image via the MPOnline "Template Maker" tool and upload it
  4. Pay the examination fee online (₹500 General/non-MP; ₹250 SC/ST/OBC/EWS residents of MP) plus MPOnline portal charges
  5. Submit the form and download/print the confirmation page for your records

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is there negative marking in the MP Police Constable exam?

No — each of the 100 questions is worth 1 mark with no penalty for incorrect answers, so attempting every question is generally advisable.

What's the minimum score needed to pass the written exam?

Reported fixed qualifying marks are 33% for General category candidates and 23% for Reserved category candidates — but confirm this against the current ESB notification since it was found on limited sources.

What happens if I fail the PET/PST even after scoring well in the written exam?

You'll be disqualified regardless of your written exam score — PET and PST are qualifying stages, and failing either (e.g., missing the height/chest standard or the 800m running time/long jump minimum) ends your candidacy for that recruitment cycle.

Does my MP domicile status affect the application fee?

Yes — reduced fees and category reservation benefits (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) apply only to candidates who are permanent residents of Madhya Pradesh; candidates from other states are treated as Unreserved/General regardless of their category elsewhere.
