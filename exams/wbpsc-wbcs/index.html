Exam Pattern

Three stages — Preliminary (objective/OMR, single paper split into subject sections), Main (descriptive, 8 papers), Personality Test/Interview. · Preliminary: 2.5 hours (150 minutes) single sitting. Mains: 3 hours per paper (8 papers across multiple days). Interview: not fixed duration.
Preliminary - single objective paper covering English Composition, General Science, Current Events, History of India, Geography (India/West Bengal focus), Indian Polity & Economy, Indian National Movement, Environmental Ecology & Biodiversity200 questions total (25 questions/25 marks per subject area), 200 marks, screening only — not counted in final merit

Mains Compulsory Paper 6 - Arithmetic and Test of Reasoning200 marks, 3 hours
Mains Optional Papers (Groups A & B candidates only, not required for Groups C/D)2 papers x 200 marks = 400 marks combined, 3 hours each
Interview - Personality TestGroup A: 200 marks, Group B: 200 marks, Group C: 150 marks, Group D: 100 marks
Negative MarkingPreliminary: approximately 1/3 mark deducted per wrong MCQ answer (exact fraction not confirmed to more precision than "~1/3" across sources). Mains: sources disagree — one secondary source claims negative marking applies to Mains as well, but Mains is a descriptive/written exam, which is inconsistent with typical negative-marking mechanics; treat "no negative marking in Mains" as the more plausible reading and this point as unconfirmed pending the official notification.

Fee and pattern details are secondary-sourced; the mains negative-marking claim and the exact Preliminary qualifying-section mechanics are inconsistent/unclear across sources and need verification against the official WBPSC WBCS notification PDF before publishing as settled fact.

How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Register/log in on the WBPSC online application portal at psc.wb.gov.in
  2. Select "WBCS (Exe.) etc. Examination, 2025-26" and fill personal, educational and category details
  3. Upload scanned photograph and signature in the specified format
  4. Pay the application fee online (₹210 + service charges for General/OBC/EWS; SC/ST/PwD candidates domiciled in West Bengal are fee-exempt)
  5. Use the edit/correction window to fix errors before it closes
  6. Download the admit card closer to the Preliminary exam date

Frequently Asked Questions

Do West Bengal domicile SC/ST candidates really pay zero application fee?

Yes, per current secondary sources — SC/ST and PwD candidates who are West Bengal domiciles are fully exempted from the WBCS application fee, but the same categories from outside West Bengal must pay the full General-category fee.

How many compulsory versus optional papers are there in WBCS Mains?

Six compulsory papers (a regional language, English, two General Studies papers, Indian Constitution & Economy, and Arithmetic/Reasoning) for everyone, plus two additional optional-subject papers only for candidates applying to Group A and Group B posts — Group C and D aspirants skip the optional papers.

Does the Preliminary exam score count toward my final WBCS rank?

No, the Preliminary exam is a screening/qualifying stage only — your final merit and rank are decided by Mains plus Interview marks.

Why do the Interview marks differ depending on which WBCS group I'm applying for?

WBCS recruits for four service groups (A, B, C, D) with different seniority/responsibility levels, and the interview weightage scales with that — Group A and B carry 200 interview marks each, while Group C carries 150 and Group D carries 100.
