Eligibility

AgeGeneral category: approx. 18–23/24 years (male), 18–28/29 years (female); OBC/MBC/EWS/SC/ST: up to ~29 years (male), ~34 years (female); widows/divorcees up to 35 years; ex-servicemen up to 42 years — as on the notified cutoff date. Exact cutoffs vary slightly by source and by post (GD/Band/Telecom vs Driver, which allows a somewhat higher upper limit); verify against the official notification for your specific category and post.
Age RelaxationSC/ST/OBC/MBC candidates and general-category women: +5 yrs · SC/ST/OBC/MBC women: +10 yrs · State government employees: +3 yrs · An additional 1-year relaxation applies to all candidates under Rule 11(3) of the Rajasthan Police Subordinate Services Rules, 1989.
QualificationSenior Secondary (12th class) or equivalent from a recognized board for General Constable; Constable Driver/Telecom roles required 12th with Science (Physics/Maths/Computer) plus a valid LMV/HMV driving licence issued at least one year before 01 Jan 2026. A qualifying Rajasthan CET (Senior Secondary Level) 2024 score was mandatory to apply. Physical standards (as reported by secondary sources, not independently verified): general-category male minimum height 168cm, chest 81cm unexpanded / 86cm expanded; female minimum height 152cm, weight ≥47.5kg; vision 6/6 without spectacles; no colour blindness, knock-knee, flat foot, stammering or similar disqualifying conditions. Relaxed standards apply for SC/ST and certain hill-area/Sahariya candidates per rules.

How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Register/log in on the Rajasthan recruitment portal (recruitment2.rajasthan.gov.in) using your SSO ID; a qualifying CET (Senior Secondary Level) score was a precondition to apply.
  2. Select the Rajasthan Police Constable Recruitment 2025 application link.
  3. Fill in personal, educational and category details as per your CET/SSO profile.
  4. Upload photograph, signature and required certificates in the prescribed format.
  5. Pay the application fee online (₹600 for General/creamy-layer OBC/MBC/out-of-state applicants; ₹400 for Rajasthan non-creamy-layer OBC/MBC/EWS/SC/ST/TSP/Saharia applicants) — CET-registered candidates were exempted from a further fee.
  6. Submit the form and print the confirmation page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a fixed passing percentage for the Rajasthan Police Constable written exam?

Yes — unlike many other state police exams that use pure merit cutoffs, Rajasthan sets fixed minimum qualifying percentages: 40% for General/OBC/MBC, 36% for SC/ST, 30% for TSP-area candidates, and 25% for Saharia (Baran) candidates.

How much negative marking is there, and does it change my strategy?

0.25 marks (a quarter mark) is deducted for every wrong answer, so guessing randomly on questions you're unsure about can hurt more than skipping them, especially since you need a fixed percentage (not just a merit rank) to pass.

What are the PET running requirements, and are they the same for everyone?

The standard requirement is a 5 km run in 25 minutes for men and 35 minutes for women, but there are relaxed timings (30 minutes) for ex-servicemen, Saharia candidates from Baran, and SC/ST candidates from TSP areas.

Do PST/PET marks add to my final selection score?

No — PST and PET are qualifying stages only; you must meet the height/chest/running standards to proceed, but they don't add marks to your merit, which is based on the written exam score (subject to the fixed qualifying percentage above).
