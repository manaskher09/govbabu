ⓘ Data note

All figures above (vacancy counts, dates, age band, qualification requirement, fee and exam scheme) were read directly from HSSC's official Advertisement No. 04/2026 PDF (hssc.gov.in), fetched and read in full, including its Appendix-A post-wise vacancy table. This is a combined "CET Phase-II" notification spanning 27 different post/department combinations, restricted to candidates who already cleared HSSC's base CET (Advt. 01/2025) — HSSC runs several such Phase-II notifications through the year (e.g. Advt. 02/2026 covering Draftsman/Mechanic-type posts at a much smaller scale, Advt. 06/2026 covering Prison Department posts at 1,238 vacancies); 04/2026 was selected here as the single largest active/recently-closed one by vacancy count. The 4,227 total is the sum of all 27 post-wise category tables in Appendix-A, independently re-added and cross-checked against the Commission's own reported figure. Pay-scale rupee ranges below are NOT stated in the advertisement PDF itself — it only cites Functional Pay Level codes such as "FPL-3," "FPL-6," "FPL-7" — so the rupee figures given here are secondary-sourced from Haryana Pay Matrix references and post-specific salary reporting, cross-checked across multiple sources but not verified against an official Haryana Finance Department pay-matrix document; treat them as approximate. Photo/signature specs are likewise not given exact pixel/KB values in this PDF. The exam's negative-marking rule is unusual and worth flagging: candidates must either answer or explicitly mark a "not attempted" bubble on the OMR sheet — failing to mark either one costs a full mark, in addition to the standard -1 for a wrong answer.

How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Only candidates who already hold a qualifying HSSC CET (Advt. 01/2025) score are eligible to apply — CET is a prerequisite, not part of this advertisement
  2. Apply online through the dedicated portal (adv042026.hryssc.com, linked from hssc.gov.in) between 09 Feb 2026 and 23 Feb 2026 (11:59 PM)
  3. Select post/department preferences — a candidate not fulfilling the essential qualification for a chosen preference is not considered for it, and cannot revert to change preferences later
  4. Upload all mandatory documents: educational certificates from Matriculation onward, a recent colour photograph with white background (name and date visible on it), scanned signature, Aadhaar card copy, and category-specific certificates (Haryana Bonafide Resident certificate, DSC/OSC/BC-A/BC-B/EWS/PwD/ESM certificates as applicable) plus the prescribed undertaking (Annexure)
  5. No application fee is required for any category
  6. Submit before the closing date — no changes to submitted particulars (including category) are entertained afterwards
  7. Await intimation of the OMR-based written exam (100 MCQs, 100 marks, 1 hour 45 minutes, bilingual Hindi/English) — minimum qualifying marks are 50% for General category and 40% for reserved categories (both vertical and horizontal)

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there negative marking for wrong answers in HSSC CET Phase-II?

No — based on available sources, wrong answers are not penalized, but leaving a question blank/unattempted costs you 1 mark, so guessing is effectively encouraged over skipping.

What is the minimum score needed to qualify?

A fixed qualifying threshold applies — General category candidates need at least 50% of total marks, while SC/BCA/BCB/EWS candidates need at least 40%, though the final merit cutoff for a specific post can be higher than this minimum.

Do all Group C posts have the same question paper?

No — the paper differs slightly for "Group 1" and "Group 2" post categories, with somewhat different question splits across similar subjects (Haryana GK, reasoning, quantitative aptitude, English/Hindi, computer, etc.).

Do I need to clear a separate CET before this exam?

Yes — CET Phase-II for Group C is a subject-knowledge-style test taken after qualifying Haryana's base Common Eligibility Test (CET), so only already-CET-qualified candidates are considered.
