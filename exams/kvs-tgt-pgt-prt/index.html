📝 How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Register and apply online via the KVS/CBSE recruitment portal linked from kvsangathan.nic.in during the notified window (14 Nov – 04 Dec 2025 for this cycle)
  2. Fill in personal, educational and category details, and select post/subject preference
  3. Upload photograph and signature per the on-screen specification
  4. Pay the application fee online (amount not independently confirmed — check the live notification)
  5. Preview, submit, and download the confirmation page
  6. Appear for Tier-1 (screening CBT), then Tier-2 (subject-specific CBT, objective + descriptive) and, for TGT/PGT, an Interview + Demo Lesson round (PRT selection is Tier-1 + Tier-2 only, no interview)

🧭 Selection Process

Two-tier online CBT (OMR-based screening in Tier-1;Tier-2 is subject knowledge test with objective + descriptive components for some posts), followed by Interview/Demo Teaching (weighted ~15% of final selection, Tier-2 ~85%).

📋 Exam Pattern

Tier-1: 2 hours. Tier-2: varies by post — approx 2.5 to 3 hours (PRT/TGT/PGT). · Negative Marking: Tier-1: 1 mark deducted per wrong answer (1/3rd of the 3 marks awarded per question). Tier-2: 0.25 marks deducted per wrong objective answer. · Passing Marks: N/A — merit-based cutoff, declared after each exam, no fixed qualifying %. Tier-1 is only a 1:10 screening ratio to shortlist for Tier-2, not a fixed qualifying percentage.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does the KVS exam have negative marking in both stages?

Yes — Tier-1 (screening) deducts 1 mark per wrong answer, and Tier-2 (subject knowledge) deducts 0.25 marks per wrong answer, based on secondary sources; this should be confirmed against the specific year's official notification since it has changed across cycles.

Is clearing Tier-1 enough to get selected?

No. Tier-1 is only a screening test — candidates are shortlisted for Tier-2 at roughly 1:10 of notified vacancies, and final merit is based mainly on Tier-2 plus interview/demo teaching performance, not Tier-1 marks.

Are PRT, TGT, and PGT exams identical in structure?

The Tier-1 screening pattern is broadly common across posts, but Tier-2 subject-knowledge weightage differs (e.g., PRT subject section carries fewer marks than TGT/PGT), so check the post-specific notification.

Do SC/ST candidates have to pay anything at all?

Based on available sources, SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-servicemen candidates are exempted from the exam fee itself but still need to pay a Rs. 500 processing fee — confirm this in the specific year's notification as fee structures are revised each cycle.
