📝 How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) on ssc.gov.in with mobile, email, Aadhaar/ID and Class 10 details
  2. Log in with your OTR credentials and fill the CGL application form
  3. Select up to 3 exam centres and your category/post preferences
  4. Upload your photo and signature to spec
  5. Pay the fee online — ₹100 for General/OBC/EWS; free for women, SC, ST, PwBD, Ex-servicemen
  6. Review and submit — download the confirmation page

📈 Career Path

Not an official timeline — a commonly cited estimate.

  • Inspector / ASO level → Superintendent / Officer grade — around 5–7 years
  • Superintendent / Officer → Assistant-Commissioner-equivalent — around 12–17 years
  • Further promotions happen through seniority, departmental exams, and Limited Departmental Competitive Exams (LDCE)

🧭 Selection Process

Computer-Based Test (CBT/online), two tiers — Tier I (screening) and Tier II (main).

📋 Exam Pattern

Tier I: 60 minutes. Tier II Paper I: 120 minutes (2 hours), with sectional/module time-locks inside. · Negative Marking: Tier I: -0.50 per wrong answer. Tier II: -1 mark per wrong answer in Section I, Section II, and the Computer Knowledge module of Section III; -0.50 per wrong answer in Paper II (JSO-specific paper). No negative marking on the DEST/typing component. · Passing Marks: N/A — merit-based/normalized ranking, no fixed qualifying %. (Candidates must, however, clear a minimum qualifying score set separately each year per category in Tier I to be shortlisted for Tier II — this is a variable, not a fixed universal percentage.)

SectionDetails
Tier I (single paper, 4 sections)100 questions, 200 marks total — General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude, English Comprehension, 25 questions/50 marks each, each section under its own 15-minute time-lock

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is Tier I of SSC CGL just a qualifying round, or does its score count toward the final merit?

Tier I is used only to shortlist candidates for Tier II — the final selection merit is built from Tier II (Paper I, plus Paper II only for Junior Statistical Officer aspirants).

Why does each section in Tier I have its own 15-minute limit instead of one 60-minute pool?

SSC locks each 25-question section to exactly 15 minutes so candidates can't spend extra time on a strong section at the expense of a weak one — you must move on when the section timer ends even if time remains unused.

Do I need to clear the Computer Knowledge and typing modules separately, or do they add to my score?

They're qualifying only — you must clear the minimum standard (and, for Tax Assistant posts, hit the required typing speed), but marks from these modules are not added to your merit score.

Is the application fee different for different posts within CGL?

No — the Rs 100 fee (with the standard women/SC/ST/PwBD/ESM exemptions) applies uniformly regardless of which CGL posts you opt for; only the Tier II paper combination differs by post (JSO vs all other posts).
