How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Visit opsc.gov.in and locate the OCS 2026 (Advt. No. 05 of 2025-26) notification under Advertisements/What's New
  2. Register on the OPSC online application system
  3. Fill in personal, educational and category details
  4. Upload photograph, signature and left thumb impression as specified (exact spec unconfirmed — see note above)
  5. Pay the examination fee online — ₹700 for General/SEBC (reported as refundable for those who attempt the Preliminary exam), exempted for SC/ST and PwBD candidates
  6. Submit the application before the deadline

Frequently Asked Questions

Do the Odia and English language papers in OPSC Mains count toward my final rank?

No — both are qualifying-only papers. You must pass them, but their marks aren't added to your merit score; only the Essay, four General Studies papers, and two Optional Subject papers (1750 marks total) plus the Interview count.

Is the CSAT paper in OPSC Prelims scored for ranking, or just pass/fail?

Just pass/fail — you need a minimum 33% in Paper II (CSAT) to qualify, but only Paper I (General Studies) marks decide your actual Prelims cutoff/ranking.

How much weight does the Interview carry in OPSC OCS compared to other state PSCs?

Quite a lot — 250 marks, which is proportionally higher than many other state PSCs on this list (e.g., UPPSC's 100 or TGPSC's ~75-100), so interview preparation matters more for OPSC OCS aspirants.

Are SC/ST/PwD candidates fully exempt from the OPSC OCS application fee?

Yes, per current secondary sources — SC/ST/PwD candidates pay no application fee, while General/OBC candidates pay Rs 500.
