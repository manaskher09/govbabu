Eligibility

Age
  • UR / EWS: 18–33 years, as on 01.01.2027 — confirmed directly against the primary CEN 04/2026 notification
  • OBC-NCL: 18–36 years (+3 years relaxation)
  • SC / ST: 18–38 years (+5 years relaxation)
  • Ex-servicemen and PwBD candidates get further relaxation under RRB's standard formula
Age RelaxationOBC-NCL +3 yrs, SC/ST +5 yrs, PwBD up to +10/13/15 yrs depending on category, Ex-servicemen per standard formula — consistent with RRB's general norms.
QualificationJE / JE (IT): 3-year diploma OR BE/B.Tech in the engineering discipline mapped to the specific post/exam-group — reported streams include Mechanical, Production, Automobile, Manufacturing, Mechatronics, Industrial, Machining, Instrumentation & Control, Tools & Die Making, Electrical, Electronics, Communication, Computer Science/Engineering, Information Technology, and Civil Engineering (exact post-to-discipline mapping runs to dozens of sub-categories in the official notification — not reproduced in full here). DMS: 3-year diploma in any engineering discipline. CMA: B.Sc. with Physics and Chemistry as principal subjects, minimum 45% marks. Qualification must be completed on or before the application closing date; results-awaited candidates are not eligible per secondary sources.

Exam Pattern

Online CBT, two stages (CBT-1 screening, CBT-2 merit-based), followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination (no separate physical test for JE). · CBT-1: 90 minutes. CBT-2: 120 minutes.
CBT-1100 questions/100 marks — Mathematics, General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Awareness, General Science (screening stage; top ~15x community-wise vacancies shortlisted for CBT-2)
CBT-2150 questions/150 marks — General Awareness, Physics & Chemistry, Computer Applications & Environment (common for all branches) + Technical Ability (branch-specific, ~100 of the 150 marks, counts fully toward merit)
Negative Marking-1/3 mark per wrong answer in both CBT-1 and CBT-2; no deduction for unattempted questions.
Passing MarksQualifying percentage used for shortlisting/eligibility: UR 40%, OBC(NCL)/SC 30%, ST 25% — final selection is by normalized merit across shifts, not a fixed cutoff.

CBT structure (100Q/90min for CBT-1, 150Q/120min for CBT-2, -1/3 negative marking) and fee figures agree across multiple secondary sources (secondary sources); an official RRB CDG FAQ PDF for JE (CEN 03/2024) exists but could not be fetched directly due to a certificate hostname mismatch on rrbcdg.gov.in, so treat as secondary-sourced with moderate-high confidence.

How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Create an account on rrbapply.gov.in (Aadhaar/DigiLocker verification recommended)
  2. Select CEN 04/2026 and register with mobile number, email ID and personal details
  3. Fill the application form and choose your preferred post(s)/RRB zone(s) per the notification's option rules
  4. Live-capture your photo during the application — there is no separate upload
  5. Upload your signature to spec (35mm × 20mm, 140×60px, 30–49KB, JPG/JPEG)
  6. Upload educational/category certificates as required
  7. Pay the fee — ₹500 for UR/OBC-NCL/EWS (₹400 refunded after appearing in CBT 1) or ₹250 for SC/ST/women/PwBD/Ex-servicemen/Transgender/Minorities/EBC (fully refunded after appearing in CBT 1); refund credited only to an Aadhaar-seeded bank account
  8. Review and submit before the 13 Sep 2026 deadline; use the modification window afterward if corrections are needed
  9. Download and retain the application confirmation/registration slip for future reference

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Technical Ability section in CBT-2 the same for every engineering branch?

No, it's branch-specific — Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, and Electronics/Signal candidates each get a different technical paper matching their branch.

Do I need to clear a physical test like Group D or RPF Constable?

No, RRB JE selection is CBT-1, CBT-2, and Document Verification/Medical only — there's no PET/physical efficiency stage.

How many candidates get shortlisted from CBT-1 to CBT-2?

Typically around 15 times the community-wise vacancies notified per RRB region, based on CBT-1 merit, though the exact multiplier is set in each notification.

Does CBT-1 score count in the final merit list?

No, like most RRB exams, CBT-1 is only used for shortlisting to CBT-2; only CBT-2 marks form the final merit list.
