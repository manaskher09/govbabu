🧭 Selection Process

Three stages — Prelims (objective/OMR or online), Mains (descriptive), Interview (personality test).

📋 Exam Pattern

Prelims: 2 hours per paper (2 papers, same day). Mains: 3 hours per paper (6 papers). Interview: not fixed duration. · Negative Marking: Prelims: none — no marks deducted for incorrect answers (per secondary sources for the 2025 SSE cycle). · Passing Marks: Prelims Paper I (GS): N/A — merit-based cutoff, declared after each exam, no fixed pre-set qualifying %. Prelims Paper II (CSAT/Aptitude): fixed qualifying minimum of 33% for general-service candidates (pass/fail only, not counted in ranking).

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is there negative marking in the MPPSC Prelims exam?

No — per current secondary sources, MPPSC does not deduct marks for wrong answers in the Prelims stage, unlike UPSC and several other state PSCs.

Do I need separate registration and fee payment for Mains after clearing Prelims?

Yes. MPPSC runs Prelims and Mains as separate registration cycles with their own application fees, so clearing Prelims doesn't automatically carry your registration forward — you register and pay again for Mains.

Does the CSAT/Aptitude paper in Prelims count toward my score?

No, Paper II (General Aptitude Test) is qualifying-only — you just need 33% to pass; your actual Prelims rank is based solely on Paper I (General Studies) marks.

How many papers does the MPPSC Mains exam have, and are any of them optional-subject based?

MPPSC Mains has six papers, all compulsory General Studies/Hindi/Essay papers — unlike UPSC or BPSC, there's no optional-subject paper in the current MPPSC pattern.
