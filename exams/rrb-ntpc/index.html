Eligibility

Age
  • Undergraduate posts: 18–30 years, as on 01 Jan 2026
  • Graduate posts: commonly reported as 18–33 years, but not independently reverified this pass
Age RelaxationStandard relaxations apply for reserved categories, Ex-servicemen and PwBD per RRB's general norms
QualificationUndergraduate posts: 12th pass (10+2) or equivalent, ≥50% aggregate (relaxed for SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-servicemen); typing proficiency required for Accounts/Junior Clerk cum Typist. Graduate posts: Bachelor's degree (commonly reported, not independently reverified this pass).

Exam Pattern

Online CBT (Computer Based Test), two stages (CBT-1, CBT-2), followed by Typing Skill Test / Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT) for some posts, then Document Verification/Medical. · CBT-1: 90 minutes (120 minutes for PwBD candidates). CBT-2: 90 minutes (120 minutes for PwBD candidates).
CBT-1100 questions/100 marks total — General Awareness 40, Mathematics 30, General Intelligence & Reasoning 30 (screening stage only, marks not counted for final merit)
CBT-2120 questions/120 marks total — General Awareness 50, Mathematics 35, General Intelligence & Reasoning 35
Typing Skill Test / CBATQualifying only (post-dependent), no marks added to merit
Negative Marking-1/3 mark per wrong answer in both CBT-1 and CBT-2.
Passing MarksQualifying percentage in CBT-2 (used as eligibility cutoff, not final merit, since final selection is by normalized merit): UR/EWS 40%, OBC(NCL)/SC 30%, ST 25%.

Fee and CBT pattern figures agree across multiple secondary sources (secondary sources) — treat as high-confidence but not verified against the original RRB CEN notification PDF, which could not be fetched directly. Final selection is merit-based/normalized across shifts; the listed percentages are minimum qualifying thresholds, not the merit cutoff.

How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Create an account on rrbapply.gov.in (Aadhaar/DigiLocker verification recommended)
  2. Fill the application for your post group (Graduate or Undergraduate)
  3. Live-capture your photo during the application — there is no separate upload
  4. Upload your signature to spec
  5. Pay the fee — ₹500 for UR/OBC-NCL/EWS (₹400 refunded after appearing in CBT) or ₹250 for SC/ST/women/PwBD/Ex-servicemen/Transgender/Minorities/EBC (fully refunded after appearing in CBT); refund credited only to an Aadhaar-seeded bank account

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the CBT-1 score count towards my final merit rank?

No. CBT-1 is only a screening/shortlisting stage — only CBT-2 marks (plus typing test/CBAT where applicable) count toward the final merit list.

Will I get my application fee back if I don't show up for the exam?

No, the partial/full refund is only processed for candidates who actually appear for CBT-1, not for no-shows.

Is there a sectional cutoff within CBT-2, or just an overall one?

Only an overall qualifying percentage applies (varies by category); there is no separate minimum required per subject/section.

Do all NTPC posts require a typing test?

No — it depends on the specific post; some posts (e.g., Junior Clerk, Accounts Clerk) require a typing skill test while others (e.g., Station Master, Goods Guard) require CBAT instead.
