📝 How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Register at opportunities.rbi.org.in under the Assistant recruitment link
  2. Fill the application form with personal, educational and category details, and select preferred RBI office/centre per the notification's option rules
  3. Upload photograph, signature, left thumb impression and handwritten declaration as per the specifications above
  4. Pay the fee online — reported as ₹50 + GST for SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-servicemen (≈₹59 total) and ₹450 + GST for all others (≈₹531 total), per secondary-source reporting, not confirmed against the primary fee clause
  5. Use the post-close correction window (14–15 Mar 2026, ₹200 fee — confirmed across multiple independent secondary sources) if corrections to centre/parents' names/marks are needed
  6. Download call letters from opportunities.rbi.org.in when released for Prelims, Mains and the Language Proficiency Test (LPT)

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Are Prelims marks used in the final RBI Assistant merit list?

No — RBI Assistant Prelims is purely qualifying for shortlisting to Mains; only Mains marks (plus the qualifying Language Test) count toward final selection.

Why is the GST added to the RBI Assistant application fee?

RBI charges the base fee (Rs. 450 or Rs. 50) plus 18% GST as a statutory tax component, so the actual amount paid is slightly higher than the base figure.

Is there a Computer Knowledge section in Prelims?

No, Computer Knowledge and General Awareness are added only in Mains; Prelims covers just English, Numerical Ability, and Reasoning.

Does RBI Assistant recruitment include an interview?

No, selection is based on Prelims + Mains + a qualifying Language Proficiency Test; there is no personal interview.
