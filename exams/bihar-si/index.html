Exam Pattern

Offline OMR-based, two-stage (Prelims + Mains) · Prelims: 2 hours. Mains: 4 hours total (across 2 papers).
Prelims (single paper)100 questions / 200 marks (2 marks/question) — General Knowledge & Current Affairs
Mains Paper I (General Hindi)100 questions / 200 marks — qualifying only, not counted in final merit
Mains Paper II (General Studies, General Science, Civics, Indian History, Geography, Mathematics, Mental Ability)100 questions / 200 marks
PET (Physical Efficiency Test)Qualifying only, no marks awarded; candidates must clear PET before proceeding to PST
PST (Physical Standard Test — height, chest, weight measurement)Qualifying only, no marks awarded
Negative Marking0.2 marks (1/5th) deducted per wrong answer, in both Prelims and Mains.
Passing MarksPrelims qualifying cutoff is a minimum of 30% marks (merit-based shortlisting beyond that, typically ~20x vacancies advance to Mains). Mains: candidates must clear both papers, but Hindi paper marks are excluded from the final merit ranking (Paper II score + Prelims score typically determine merit — exact merit formula not fully confirmed). Final selection cutoff itself is merit-based and declared after each exam — no single fixed qualifying % for overall selection.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many stages does the Bihar Police SI (Daroga) selection process have?

Four: a Preliminary written exam, a Mains written exam (two papers), a Physical Efficiency Test (PET), and a Physical Standard Test (PST), followed by document verification and medical exam.

Does my Hindi paper score in the Mains count toward my final merit?

No — the Hindi paper (Paper I) is qualifying only; you must pass it, but its marks are not added into your final merit ranking, which is based on Paper II (General Studies etc.) and your Prelims performance.

Is there negative marking in the Bihar Police SI exam?

Yes, in both Prelims and Mains — 0.2 marks (1/5th mark) are deducted for every wrong answer.

What is the minimum qualifying percentage to clear the Prelims?

Candidates generally need at least 30% marks to be shortlisted for Mains, though the exact cutoff each year is also influenced by the number of vacancies and category-wise reservation.
