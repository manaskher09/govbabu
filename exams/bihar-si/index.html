🧭 Selection Process

Offline OMR-based, two-stage (Prelims + Mains)

📋 Exam Pattern

Prelims: 2 hours. Mains: 4 hours total (across 2 papers). · Negative Marking: 0.2 marks (1/5th) deducted per wrong answer, in both Prelims and Mains. · Passing Marks: Prelims qualifying cutoff is a minimum of 30% marks (merit-based shortlisting beyond that, typically ~20x vacancies advance to Mains). Mains: candidates must clear both papers, but Hindi paper marks are excluded from the final merit ranking (Paper II score + Prelims score typically determine merit — exact merit formula not fully confirmed). Final selection cutoff itself is merit-based and declared after each exam — no single fixed qualifying % for overall selection.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How many stages does the Bihar Police SI (Daroga) selection process have?

Four: a Preliminary written exam, a Mains written exam (two papers), a Physical Efficiency Test (PET), and a Physical Standard Test (PST), followed by document verification and medical exam.

Does my Hindi paper score in the Mains count toward my final merit?

No — the Hindi paper (Paper I) is qualifying only; you must pass it, but its marks are not added into your final merit ranking, which is based on Paper II (General Studies etc.) and your Prelims performance.

Is there negative marking in the Bihar Police SI exam?

Yes, in both Prelims and Mains — 0.2 marks (1/5th mark) are deducted for every wrong answer.

What is the minimum qualifying percentage to clear the Prelims?

Candidates generally need at least 30% marks to be shortlisted for Mains, though the exact cutoff each year is also influenced by the number of vacancies and category-wise reservation.
