Agniveer (Indian Army)

StatusClosed

ⓘ Data note

Agnipath is a 4-year tour-of-duty scheme distinct from pre-2022 permanent enrollment — do not confuse Agniveer terms (Seva Nidhi, 25% retention) with older regular-soldier pension/pay rules. This entry covers ARMY Agniveer only. Navy (Agniveer) and Air Force (Agniveer Vayu) run separate CEEs/portals and are comparably large in relative terms (Navy/Air Force together budget ~6,000 seats/year vs Army's ~40,000) — worth adding as separate entries later for full tri-service coverage. Applicant volume: ~12.8 lakh applicants in 2024 (up ~10% from 11.3 lakh in 2023), per Army recruitment data reported by ThePrint — Army-only, not tri-service.

QualificationVaries by category: General Duty (GD) — Class 10 pass with 45% marks aggregate and 33% per subject; Technical — Class 12 pass with 50% aggregate and 40% per subject in Physics/Chemistry/Maths and English, OR a 1-year technical course (NSQF Level 4) after Class 10; Clerk/Store Keeper Technical — Class 12 pass in any stream with 60% aggregate, 50% per subject, and 50% in English plus Maths/Accounts/Book-Keeping, plus basic computer proficiency; Tradesman — Class 10 pass with 33% marks for most trades, Class 8 pass accepted for a few specific trades (e.g. Cook, Washerman, Mess Keeper). Exam Pattern

Online CEE (Common Entrance Examination, CBT/MCQ) — one written test for all Agniveer entry streams (General Duty, Technical, Clerk/Store Keeper, Tradesman) but the number of questions/duration differs by the entry stream's education requirement — followed by Physical Fitness Test (PFT), Physical Measurement Test (PMT), Medical Exam, and Document Verification (Rally/Screening phase). · 1 hour for the 50-question format (typically General Duty/Tradesman-type entries); 2 hours for the 100-question format (typically Technical/Clerk entries requiring more subjects, e.g. Science stream).
CEE (shorter format)50 questions/100 marks, 60 minutes — General Knowledge, Mathematics, General Science, Logical Reasoning (mix and question count depends on entry category)
CEE (longer format)100 questions/200 marks, 120 minutes — same broad subjects plus additional Physics/Chemistry-heavy content for Technical entries
Physical Fitness Test (PFT)Qualifying — 1.6 km run (timed), pull-ups, 9-ft ditch jump, zig-zag balance (varies by category/group)
Physical Measurement Test (PMT)Qualifying — height, weight, chest measurement per regional/category norms
Medical ExaminationQualifying — per Army medical standards
Negative Marking+2 marks per correct answer, -0.5 mark per wrong answer (i.e., -1/4 of the marks awarded per question) — this figure is the most consistently reported, though some sources cite -0.25 flatly; treat the -0.5-per-wrong-answer figure (against +2 correct) as the better-supported one.
Passing MarksN/A — merit-based ranking; CEE score plus PFT/PMT (qualifying stages) determine final merit, with no single fixed passing percentage published for the written CEE.

Sources disagree somewhat on exact negative marking (some say -0.5 per wrong vs +2 correct, others say flat -0.25) and on whether question count is fixed at 50 or varies 50/100 by category — the 50-or-100-question, 60-or-120-minute structure by entry stream and the +2/-0.5 marking scheme were the most repeated across multiple secondary sources, so treat those as moderate confidence; fee of Rs 250 flat is corroborated by 3 independent sources but category-wise differentiation (if any, e.g. for women/ex-servicemen dependents) was not found and should be treated as NOT FOUND rather than assumed absent.

Career Progression

Official Agnipath policy.

  • After the 4-year engagement, up to 25% of each batch of Agniveers may apply for the permanent/regular cadre (based on performance, discipline and organisational requirement)
  • Those selected continue under regular Army terms — about a 15-year engagement, with further promotion eligibility
  • The remaining ~75% exit with the Seva Nidhi lump sum, a skill certificate, and priority consideration for some government/paramilitary jobs — this is priority only, not a guaranteed job

How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Register on joinindianarmy.nic.in and complete the online CEE application form (choose category — GD/Technical/Clerk-SKT/Tradesman/etc.)
  2. Upload photo and signature to the portal's current specifications (verify live — see notes above)
  3. Pay the application/exam fee via the online payment gateway
  4. Download the CEE admit card when released (typically ~2 weeks before the exam)
  5. Appear for the Common Entrance Examination (CEE) — computer-based test at designated centres
  6. Wait for the zone/ARO-wise CEE merit list to be declared
  7. Report to the Army Recruiting Office (ARO) rally venue per your merit-list category/zone for the Physical Fitness Test (PFT) — running, beam, and other events
  8. Clear the Physical Measurement Test (PMT) — height, weight, chest measurements against category-specific standards
  9. Undergo document verification at the rally site
  10. Undergo the detailed Medical Examination at Military Hospital/designated medical facility
  11. Await the final merit list combining CEE score + PFT/PMT/medical clearance
  12. Receive enrollment/joining instructions for Agniveer training at the allotted Regimental Training Centre

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Agniveer CEE the same test for every trade (General Duty, Technical, Clerk)?

No — while all use the same CEE format broadly, the number of questions (50 vs 100), duration, and subject emphasis differ depending on the entry category and its educational requirement (e.g., Technical entries lean more on Physics/Chemistry/Maths).

Does clearing the CEE guarantee selection?

No, CEE clearance only qualifies you for the Physical Fitness Test, Physical Measurement Test, and Medical Exam — final merit combines CEE marks with clearing these qualifying stages, and you can still be rejected at PFT/PMT/Medical even after a good CEE score.

Is there a document screening/rally before the CEE, or after?

The process generally begins with an online CEE, and only candidates who clear it are called for the Rally-based Physical Fitness Test, Physical Measurement Test, and Document Verification — it is not rally-first for the current CEE-based system introduced under Agnipath.

Do all four years of Agniveer service guarantee a permanent Army job afterward?

No — under the Agnipath scheme, only a fixed percentage (commonly cited as up to 25%) of each batch is selected for permanent enrolment after the 4-year term, based on performance; the rest exit with the Seva Nidhi package. (This percentage figure was not independently re-verified in this research pass — confirm against the current joinindianarmy.nic.in policy if quoting it as fact.)
