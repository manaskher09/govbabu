📝 How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Register and apply online only via www.rbi.org.in / opportunities.rbi.org.in — no other mode is accepted.
  2. Fill in personal, academic and category details, and choose up to 4 preferred exam centres each for Phase-I and Phase-II.
  3. Upload photograph, signature, left thumb impression and a handwritten declaration per the Bank's prescribed specifications (see Appendix-I on the official site).
  4. Pay the Application Fee/Intimation Charges — ₹850 + 18% GST for GEN/OBC/EWS, ₹100 + 18% GST (intimation charges only) for SC/ST/PwBD, nil for eligible RBI staff candidates.
  5. Appear for Phase-I online examination (13–14 Jun 2026 depending on cadre).
  6. Qualifiers appear for Phase-II (General: Papers I–III, 25 Jul 2026; DEPR/DSIM: Papers I–II/III, 26 Jul 2026).
  7. Successful candidates are called for a Board interview; final selection is on a combined Phase-II + interview basis, with certificates (age/qualification/category) verified only at the interview stage.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is RBI Grade B Phase 1 counted in the final merit list?

No, Phase 1 is a qualifying screening exam only; final merit is based on Phase 2 (Paper I, II, III) plus Interview marks.

Does the English paper in Phase 2 have negative marking?

No — it's a fully descriptive/typed paper (essay, precis, comprehension-based writing), so there's no negative marking on it, unlike the objective Phase 1 test.

What subjects does the Finance & Management (FM) paper cover?

It typically covers banking regulation, financial markets, risk management, corporate governance, and management/organizational behavior topics, combining objective and descriptive elements depending on the year.

How many vacancies does RBI Grade B 2026 have, and does that affect competition?

2026-cycle reports indicate a relatively small vacancy count (reported around 60 for Grade B DR-General in some 2026 sources), making this a highly competitive, low-vacancy recruitment cycle compared to bank PO/Clerk exams.
