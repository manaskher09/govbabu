📝 How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Go to www.licindia.in > Careers and click "Recruitment of AAO (Generalist/Specialists/Assistant Engineers) 2025", then choose "Click here for New Registration" and enter name, contact details and email to generate a Provisional Registration Number and Password
  2. Log back in, fill personal/educational/category details, using "Save and Next" to save progress; validate details and save the application
  3. Scan and upload photograph, signature, left thumb impression and the handwritten declaration exactly per the specifications above
  4. Preview the full application on the Preview tab before Final Submit — no changes are allowed after this step
  5. Pay the non-refundable application fee/intimation charge online (Debit/Credit Card, Net Banking, IMPS or Mobile Wallet) — ₹85 + transaction charges + GST for SC/ST/PwBD candidates, ₹700 + transaction charges + GST for all others
  6. Click Submit, then print the system-generated application form containing fee details for your records (available to reprint until 23 Sep 2025 for this cycle)
  7. Download the call letter 7 days before each exam stage from licindia.in > Careers, and appear for Preliminary Exam → Main Exam (objective + descriptive) → Interview → Pre-Recruitment Medical Examination, in that order

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does LIC AAO have negative marking like IBPS/SBI exams?

No — for Generalist posts, LIC AAO has no negative marking in either Prelims or Mains, which is a key difference from most other bank/insurance exams; Specialist posts reportedly do have negative marking in Prelims.

Are Prelims English Language marks counted toward the merit list?

No, the English Language section in Prelims is qualifying only — you must clear a minimum cutoff, but the marks themselves aren't added to your merit score.

What does the Mains Descriptive Test in LIC AAO cover?

It's a qualifying-only writing test (essay/letter-type); exact marks and duration vary by notification cycle and weren't confirmed with precision for 2026 in the sources checked.

Is LIC AAO Generalist post pattern the same as Specialist posts?

No — Specialist posts (e.g., IT, CA, Actuarial) reportedly have negative marking in Prelims where Generalist posts do not; syllabus emphasis also differs by specialization.
