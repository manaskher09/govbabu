📝 How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Step 1: Register on the UPPRPB One Time Registration (OTR) portal at uppbpb.gov.in with basic personal details, mobile number and email.
  2. Step 2: Log in and fill the Constable Civil Police 2025 application form with educational, category and physical-eligibility details.
  3. Step 3: Upload scanned photograph (3.5 cm × 4.5 cm, 20–50 KB, JPG) and signature (3.5 cm × 1.5 cm, 5–20 KB, JPG) as per the specification on the live form.
  4. Step 4: Pay the application fee online (₹500 for General/OBC/EWS; ₹400 for SC/ST, per secondary reporting) via the payment gateway.
  5. Step 5: Review and submit the form; download/print the confirmation page for records.
  6. Step 6: Await written exam (PBT) admit card, appear for the exam, then proceed through Document Verification (DV), Physical Standard Test (PST) and Physical Efficiency Test (PET) — PST/PET standards: male height ≥168 cm (Gen/OBC/SC), ≥160 cm (ST), chest 79–84 cm (Gen/OBC)/77–82 cm (SC) with expansion, weight ≥50 kg, 4.8 km run in 25 min; female height ≥152 cm (Gen/OBC/SC), ≥147 cm (ST), no chest measurement, weight ≥40 kg, 2.4 km run in 14 min — per secondary reporting, not independently confirmed against an official PST/PET standards PDF.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is there negative marking in the UP Police Constable exam?

For the 2026 recruitment cycle, UP government reportedly abolished negative marking via a late-2025 rule amendment — each correct answer is worth 2 marks and wrong answers are not penalized. This is a recent change, so double-check your specific notification/admit card instructions before the exam.

Do the physical test (PST/PET) marks count toward my final merit, or are they just qualify/disqualify?

They are qualifying only — you must clear the height/chest/weight standards (PST) and the running requirement (PET) to proceed, but your final merit rank is decided by written exam marks, not physical test performance.

What running distance and time do I need to clear PET?

Male candidates must run 4.8 km within 25 minutes; female candidates must run 2.4 km within 14 minutes. Missing the cutoff disqualifies you regardless of your written exam score.

Is the UP Police Constable written exam online (computer-based) or offline (pen-and-paper OMR)?

It is an offline OMR-sheet-based test, not a computer-based test — you mark answers by hand on an OMR sheet, which is later scanned for evaluation.
