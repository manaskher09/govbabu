Eligibility

AgeNot exceeding 30 years, determined as on the crucial/cut-off date of 15 Jul 2026 (the advertisement's closing date).
Age RelaxationSC/ST +5 yrs · OBC +3 yrs · PwBD +10 yrs · PwBD+SC/ST +15 yrs · PwBD+OBC +13 yrs · Ex-servicemen: period of military service + 3 yrs (max 55 yrs) · Meritorious sportspersons +5 yrs (+10 SC/ST, +8 OBC, Group C posts only) · Government servants: relaxable up to 40 yrs of age (45 SC/ST, 43 OBC) for Group C posts in the same/allied cadre.
QualificationBachelor's degree in Computer Application (BCA) from a recognized university, OR Graduation in Computer Science (with Computer Science studied as a main subject in all three years), OR BE/B.Tech in Computer Science/Information Technology, OR Graduation in any subject plus an 'A' level course from DOEACC (Ministry of Electronics & IT). Qualifications are relaxable at the Competent Authority's discretion for otherwise well-qualified candidates.

How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Register once on the DSSSB portal at dsssbonline.nic.in (a one-time exercise; the same login is used for all future DSSSB exams)
  2. Log in and select the examination for post code 41/26 (TGT Computer Science) under Advertisement No. 03/2026
  3. Fill in personal, educational and category details, and select category/reservation if applicable
  4. Upload photograph and signature per the on-screen portal specification (plain white/off-white background, full face, eyes open)
  5. Pay the ₹100 application fee via SBI e-Pay only (women, SC/ST, PwBD and Ex-servicemen candidates are exempt from the fee)
  6. Review and submit before the 15 Jul 2026, 11:59 PM deadline — no changes are allowed after submission, and no offline/postal applications are accepted
  7. Await the One Tier (Technical/Teaching) computer-based exam call letter — Section A (100 MCQs: General Awareness, Reasoning, Arithmetic, Hindi & English, 100 marks) + Section B (100 MCQs on the subject and teaching methodology/B.Ed, 100 marks), 2 hours total, with 0.25-mark negative marking per wrong answer

Frequently Asked Questions

How much weightage does the actual Computer Science subject knowledge carry versus general sections?

Roughly half the paper (100 of 200 marks) is the "Subject Concerned" section covering CS subject knowledge and teaching methodology/B.Ed pedagogy, with the remaining 100 marks split across General Awareness, Reasoning, Numerical Ability, English, and Hindi.

Is there any interview after the CBT?

DSSSB TGT posts are typically filled through the CBT-based merit list alone, without a separate personal interview, but always check the specific post's recruitment notification since this can vary.

Who is exempt from the application fee?

SC, ST, PwD (PwBD), women, sportspersons, and ex-servicemen candidates are exempted from the Rs. 100 fee; everyone else pays via SBI e-Pay only.

Is there sectional cutoff or only an overall cutoff?

No fixed sectional qualifying marks were confirmed; DSSSB recruitment is generally merit-based on the overall CBT score, with category-wise cutoffs declared after the exam rather than a pre-fixed qualifying percentage.
