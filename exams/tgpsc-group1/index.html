IMPORTANT: the Telangana State Public Service Commission was officially renamed TGPSC (Telangana Public Service Commission) following the state's abbreviation change from TS to TG — older "TSPSC" branding and the tspsc.gov.in domain are being phased out. A live check of TGPSC's own homepage (04 Sep 2026) confirms there is currently no open Group-I, II, III or IV notification — the most recent postings are smaller specialist recruitments (Town Planning roles), not a Group exam. Several 2026-dated articles claiming a fresh "563-post Group-I 2026 notification" turned out on cross-check to be recycled coverage of the 2024 cycle (Notification No. 02/2024) with the year mislabeled — this entry describes that real, completed 2024 cycle instead, since no current cycle exists. The official 2024 notification PDF could not be directly fetched (DNS/network errors), so vacancy count and dates are secondary-sourced (Sakshi Education and other secondary sources) though reasonably well-corroborated across independent sources; age relaxation, category-wise fee, and photo/signature specs are weak/unconfirmed and should not be relied on. A notable, fairly specific detail from two independent secondary sources: the Interview stage was removed for the 2024 Group-I cycle, with final merit based on Mains score plus document verification only.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there an interview for TGPSC Group 1 selection?

This is unsettled across sources — recent secondary reporting on the 2024 Group-1 cycle indicates selection was based on Prelims + Mains only (no interview), while other sources describe a roughly 75-mark interview stage. Telangana has changed its stance on group-service interviews before, so check the specific notification you're targeting.

Does the Prelims score count toward the final TGPSC Group 1 merit list?

No — Prelims (150 questions/150 marks) is a qualifying screening stage only. Your final rank is decided by Mains (900 merit-counted marks across 6 papers) plus Interview marks, if applicable for your cycle.

Is the General English paper in Mains compulsory, and does it count toward merit?

It's compulsory to clear (Class 10 standard difficulty) but is qualifying-only — its 150 marks are not added to your merit score, unlike Papers I-VI.

Why did the TGPSC application fee jump so much recently?

TGPSC raised its processing fee roughly five-fold between the 2024 and 2025 cycles (from around Rs 200 to Rs 1,000 for General/BC candidates), a change that drew notable criticism from aspirants — check the live notification for the exact current fee rather than assuming either older figure still applies.
