Posts & Pay

Level 4 · ₹25,500–₹81,100Postal Assistant / Sorting Assistant, Data Entry Operator, Data Entry Operator Grade A
Level 2 · ₹19,900–₹63,200Lower Division Clerk / Junior Secretariat Assistant

Figures are 7th CPC pay-matrix bands (basic pay only) — actual in-hand adds Dearness Allowance (currently ~58% of basic) and House Rent Allowance (8–24% by city class).

Eligibility

Age18–27 years as on the cutoff date set by each cycle's notification (exact birth-date bounds change every cycle) — confirm current bounds on ssc.gov.in.
Age RelaxationStandard SSC relaxations are expected to apply (OBC +3 yrs · SC/ST +5 yrs · PwBD +10 yrs) — not independently reconfirmed for CHSL specifically.
QualificationClass 12 (10+2) pass from a recognized board (Data Entry Operator, Grade A posts require Mathematics as a 12th-standard subject).

Career Progression

Not an official timeline — a commonly cited estimate.

  • LDC / JSA → Upper Division Clerk (UDC) / Senior Secretariat Assistant — around 5–7 years (by seniority, sometimes a departmental exam)
  • Separately, all central government employees get a pay-level upgrade (MACP) after 10, 20 and 30 years of service, whether or not they've actually been promoted

How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) on ssc.gov.in, if not already done for another SSC exam
  2. Log in and fill the CHSL-specific application — post preference, exam centres, category
  3. Upload your photo and signature to spec
  4. Pay the fee online — ₹100 for General/OBC/EWS; free for women, SC, ST, PwD, Ex-servicemen
  5. Review, submit, and save your confirmation page
