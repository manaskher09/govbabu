📝 How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) on the JPSC OTR portal (reported as jpscotr.com, reached via jpsc.gov.in — not independently verified)
  2. Fill the CCE application form referencing your OTR ID
  3. Upload photograph and signature as specified (see caveats above)
  4. Pay the examination fee online — reported as ₹100 General/OBC/EWS, ₹50 SC/ST, exempt for PwD (figures unverified against the official notice)
  5. Submit the application and print the confirmation

🧭 Selection Process

Two stages of relevance for merit — Preliminary (objective, screening only) and Main (descriptive) plus Interview.

📋 Exam Pattern

Prelims: 2 papers totaling 4 hours (2 hours each, same day). Mains: paper-wise duration not confirmed from sources found (commonly 3 hours per paper for similar state PSCs, but not verified for JPSC specifically). Interview: approximately 30 minutes per candidate per some sources. · Negative Marking: DISPUTED — sources directly conflict. This could not be resolved with the searches performed; treat as unconfirmed and verify against the specific CCE notification year before publishing. ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does JPSC Prelims have negative marking?

This is unclear — secondary sources disagree, with some saying there's no negative marking and others citing a 1/3-mark deduction per wrong answer. Check the specific CCE notification you're preparing for, since JPSC's rules may also have changed across recent cycles.

Does JPSC Mains have an optional subject paper like BPSC or UPSC?

No — per current secondary sources, JPSC CCE Mains consists of six compulsory papers with no optional-subject choice, unlike BPSC (34 optional subjects) or the old UPSC pattern.

How long is the JPSC Prelims exam?

It consists of two papers held the same day, together totaling 4 hours (2 hours per paper) — longer than many other state PSC prelims exams on this list.

Do Prelims marks count toward the final JPSC merit list?

No — Prelims is purely a screening stage to shortlist candidates for Mains; only Mains and Interview marks decide your final rank.
