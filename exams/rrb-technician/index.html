📝 How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Create an account on the RRB CEN portal via the link on your chosen RRB's official website (reached through rrbapply.gov.in) — mobile number and email become permanent once submitted
  2. Log in, choose your exam medium, and fill the online application — one separate application is required per pay level (Grade-I Signal and Grade-III need separate applications, since they sit separate CBTs)
  3. Choose only one RRB per pay level and rank your post preferences within it
  4. Live-capture your photograph via webcam/phone camera when prompted — no file upload option exists
  5. Upload your scanned signature (JPG/JPEG, 30–49 KB, min. 140×60 px) and, if claiming a free travel pass, your SC/ST certificate as a PDF under 400 KB
  6. Pay the applicable fee online, separately for each pay level applied to
  7. Use the modification window if corrections are needed (extra fee applies; your RRB choice and account details cannot be changed)
  8. Await CBT city/date/shift allotment — announced later; no requests for date, venue or shift change are entertained
  9. Proceed through CBT, Document Verification and Medical Examination in that order, by merit

🧭 Selection Process

Online CBT — single stage for most Technician posts (Grade III), Document Verification and Medical Examination follow; no PET/physical test.

📋 Exam Pattern

CBT: 90 minutes (120 minutes for PwBD candidates). · Negative Marking: -1/3 mark per wrong answer; no deduction for unattempted/blank questions. · Passing Marks: N/A — merit-based/normalized ranking across shifts; no fixed qualifying percentage confirmed for this post in the sources checked.

SectionDetails
CBT100 questions/100 marks — Mathematics, General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Science, General Awareness (Technician Grade I / Signal posts additionally include a Technical/discipline-specific component within or alongside this CBT)

Fee structure and CBT pattern (100Q/100 marks/90 min, -1/3 negative marking) agree across multiple secondary sources (secondary sources, moderate-high confidence).An official CEN 02/2025 PDF for Technician categories was located at rrbsecunderabad.gov.in but not opened directly — treat exact section-wise split and any qualifying percentage as unconfirmed pending that document.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a separate CBT-2 for Technician like there is for NTPC or JE?

No, most Technician posts (Grade III) have only a single-stage CBT; Grade I/Signal posts may have additional technical requirements but sources do not confirm a distinct second CBT stage.

Do Technician posts require a physical test like Group D?

No, based on available sources Technician recruitment does not include a PET — selection is CBT followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination.

Are the fee and refund rules the same as other RRB exams (NTPC, Group D)?

Yes, the Rs 500/Rs 250 fee structure with partial/full refund on appearing for CBT is consistent with other RRB-conducted recruitment.

Does Technician Grade I (Signal) have a different, harder exam than Grade III?

Sources indicate Grade I/Signal includes an additional technical-discipline component, making it more specialized than Grade III's general-subjects-only paper, but the exact question split was not independently confirmed here.
