📝 How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Create an account on rrbapply.gov.in (Aadhaar/DigiLocker verification recommended)
  2. Select CEN 01/2026 (ALP) and register with mobile number, email ID and personal details
  3. Fill the application form, choosing preferred RRB zone(s) per the notification's option rules
  4. Live-capture your photo during the application — there is no separate upload
  5. Upload your signature to spec (35mm × 20mm, 140×60px, 30–49KB, JPG/JPEG, cursive black ink)
  6. Upload ITI/diploma and category certificates as required
  7. Pay the fee — ₹500 for UR/OBC-NCL/EWS (₹400 refunded after appearing in CBT 1) or ₹250 for SC/ST/women/PwBD/Ex-servicemen/Transgender/Minorities/EBC (fully refunded after appearing in CBT 1); refund credited only to an Aadhaar-seeded bank account
  8. Review and submit before the 14 Jun 2026 deadline
  9. Download and retain the application confirmation/registration slip; watch rrbapply.gov.in and your regional RRB site for CBT 1 city/date intimation

📈 Career Path

Not addressed in the official notification — coaching-site consensus, not RRB-confirmed.

  • Assistant Loco Pilot → Loco Pilot (Goods) — after a minimum of about 2 years' service and about 60,000 km of running experience as an Assistant
  • Loco Pilot (Goods) → Loco Pilot (Mail/Express) → Senior Loco Pilot → Loco Inspector / Loco Supervisor
  • Departmental exams can also open routes to Power Controller, Crew Controller, or Loco Foreman
  • Reaching senior driving roles (e.g. Rajdhani/Shatabdi links) is commonly cited as taking 8–10+ years overall

🧭 Selection Process

Online CBT, two stages (CBT-1 screening, CBT-2 merit-based with Part A + Part B), followed by CBAT (Computer Based Aptitude Test, qualifying only), Document Verification and Medical Examination.

📋 Exam Pattern

CBT-1: 60 minutes. CBT-2 Part A: 90 minutes, Part B: 60 minutes. (PwBD candidates get extra time per rules.) · Negative Marking: -1/3 (0.33) mark per wrong answer in CBT-1 and CBT-2 (Parts A and B); no negative marking in CBAT. · Passing Marks: N/A — merit-based/normalized ranking for CBT-2 Part A; Part B (Trade test) has a fixed qualifying threshold of 35% for all categories, but does not itself contribute marks to merit.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

If I score well in CBT-2 Part A but do poorly in the trade test (Part B), will I still be selected?

Only if you clear the minimum 35% qualifying mark in Part B — if you fall short there, your Part A marks are not considered at all regardless of how high they are.

Does CBAT actually affect my final merit rank?

No, CBAT is purely qualifying (pass/fail) for ALP; it does not add marks to your final merit score, unlike CBT-2 Part A.

Can I choose which trade syllabus to prepare for CBT-2 Part B?

No, the trade paper is fixed based on the trade option you selected at the time of application, so choose that option carefully before applying.

Is there negative marking in the CBAT stage?

No, CBAT has no negative marking — it is scored purely as qualify/disqualify.
