Eligibility

Age
  • Minimum age: 20, 21 or 22 years depending on post, as on 01 Aug 2026
  • Maximum age: 37 years (General male), 40 years (General female / EBC / BC), 42 years (SC/ST)
  • A PwD relaxation up to 47 appears in only one source — not independently confirmed
Age RelaxationSee age ranges above; women get an additional relaxation on the general maximum.
QualificationBachelor's degree, any discipline, from a recognized university. Bihar domicile is not required to apply — only to claim Bihar-specific reservation benefits.

Career Progression

Unverified against a primary source — treat as an estimate.

  • BPSC-to-IAS promotion via the State Civil Service quota is commonly cited as notably longer than other state PSCs — around 15–20 years

How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Complete registration at bpsconline.bihar.gov.in
  2. Fill your profile, education and experience details
  3. Capture your photo live via webcam during the application — no separate upload
  4. Upload two signature images — one Hindi, one English — to spec
  5. Submit and pay the fee

Other Documents

Physical copieskeep 5 copies of your current photograph on hand — may be required later in the process
