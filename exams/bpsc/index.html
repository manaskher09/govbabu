Exam Pattern

Three stages — Prelims (objective/OMR or online CBT MCQ), Mains (descriptive, pen-and-paper), Interview (personality test). · Prelims: 2 hours (one paper). Mains: each of the 5 papers is a separate 3-hour paper (held on different days). Interview: not fixed duration.
Prelims - General Studies (single paper, MCQ)150 questions, 150 marks, 2 hours, screening-only (marks not added to final merit)
Mains - General Hindi100 marks, 3 hours, qualifying only (min 30% needed, not counted in merit)
Mains - General Studies Paper I300 marks, 3 hours, counted in merit
Mains - General Studies Paper II300 marks, 3 hours, counted in merit
Mains - Essay300 marks, 3 hours, counted in merit
Mains - Optional Subject100 marks, 2 hours, qualifying only (category-wise minimum: General 40%, BC 36.5%, EBC 34%, SC/ST/Women/PwD 32%) — not counted in merit
Interview - Personality Test120 marks, counted in merit
Negative MarkingPrelims: -1/3 (0.33) mark deducted per wrong answer (this 1/3 penalty applies from the 71st CCE onward per multiple sources; earlier cycles used a different scheme — verify against the specific CCE notification year you are covering). Mains (descriptive papers): no negative marking (not an MCQ format apart from the optional paper, which is MCQ-based but sources did not confirm a specific negative-marking value for it — treat as unconfirmed).
Passing MarksPrelims: N/A — merit-based cutoff, declared after each exam, no fixed pre-set qualifying %. (Mains Hindi paper has a fixed 30% qualifying mark, and the Mains Optional paper has fixed category-wise qualifying percentages — General 40%, BC 36.5%, EBC 34%, SC/ST/Women/PwD 32% — but these are qualifying-only papers, not the main Prelims/Mains merit cutoff.)

Fee and pattern figures are secondary-sourced and describe the 70th/71st/72nd CCE cycles; BPSC has changed prelims negative-marking and mains structure across recent cycles, so double-check the exact CCE number's official notification PDF before publishing as current.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BPSC Prelims marks counted in the final merit list?

No. BPSC Prelims is purely a screening/qualifying stage — only Mains and Interview marks count toward the final merit and rank.

Do I need to clear the optional subject paper in Mains to get selected?

Yes, but only as a qualifying hurdle — you must score the minimum category-wise percentage in the optional paper, but the marks themselves are not added to your merit score. Only GS-I, GS-II, and Essay marks (plus Interview) decide your final rank.

Is there negative marking in the BPSC Mains exam?

The Mains papers (Hindi, GS-I, GS-II, Essay) are descriptive/subjective, so there's no negative marking there. Only the objective Prelims paper has negative marking.

Does BPSC have a fixed passing percentage for Prelims like some other state PSCs?

No. BPSC Prelims cutoffs are announced fresh after each exam based on difficulty and number of vacancies — there's no pre-declared fixed qualifying percentage (unlike the Mains Hindi and Optional papers, which do have fixed qualifying minimums).
