Kerala PSC LD Clerk (LDC)

Clerk — Cat.No.503/2023 (Part I, Direct Recruitment) — Kerala PSC's combined Lower Division Clerk (LDC) recruitment in Various Departments across all 14 districts, including the integrated Clerk/Village Assistant post in the Revenue Department.This is Kerala PSC's highest-volume clerical recruitment.The application window closed in Jan 2024; the resulting Ranked List was published 01 Aug 2025 and is currently in force (valid till 2028), with fresh vacancies still being reported and candidates actively appointed from it district-by-district through 2026.No new LDC application window is open right now.

ⓘ Data note

Kerala PSC does not run one big annual LDC exam with a declared total vacancy count — it issues a direct-recruitment notification (this one, Cat.No.503/2023, Gazette 30 Nov 2023) for the LD Clerk post across all departments, holds one SSLC-standard objective (OMR) test per district, and then uses the resulting Ranked List for years to fill "anticipated vacancies" as they actually arise, rather than a fixed number being declared upfront.The OMR test for this notification was held 27 Jul 2024, and the resulting Ranked List (No.599/2025/DOT) came into force 01 Aug 2025 — valid until 2028 unless exhausted sooner — and is the currently operative LDC list, with appointment charts still being issued district-by-district into 2026 (charts dated as late as Apr 2026 were found on keralapsc.gov.in).The prior LDC rank list (which expired 31 Jul 2025) had produced roughly 12,238 appointments across all 14 districts over its life — cited here only as scale context, not as this cycle's vacancy count.No new LDC application window is open as of Sep 2026, and the next fresh LDC notification is not expected before 2027.This entry's core facts (post, pay scale, age window, qualification, application mechanics) were read directly from the official notification PDF on keralapsc.gov.in, which is a stronger source than most of the secondary exam-prep sites available for Kerala PSC — but photo/signature specs beyond what the notification itself states could not be verified, and are flagged as such above.

📝 How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Complete One Time Registration (OTR) on keralapsc.gov.in if not already registered
  2. Log in to your OTR profile with your User-ID and password
  3. Click "Apply Now" against the relevant notification/post and select your preferred district (a separate application is needed for each district/category chosen)
  4. Verify all profile details (photo, qualification, community, etc.) for correctness — no application fee is charged
  5. Submit the application — it becomes provisional and cannot be edited or deleted afterward
  6. Once the exam calendar is published, log back in to confirm your intent to write the test and download the admission ticket during the specified window

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is there an interview for the Kerala PSC LD Clerk post?

No. LDC selection is based entirely on your OMR written-exam score and the resulting district-wise rank list — there's no separate interview round, unlike some other state PSC exams.

Does Kerala PSC LDC charge any application fee?

No, Kerala PSC generally does not charge an application fee for LDC or most of its other recruitment exams — you just need a valid Kerala PSC profile (One Time Registration) to apply.

How much time do I have per question in the LDC exam?

You get 75 minutes for 100 questions, which is roughly 45 seconds per question — tight enough that negative marking (-0.33 per wrong answer) makes guessing risky unless you can eliminate at least 2 of 4 options.

Is the same LDC rank list used to fill vacancies across all districts of Kerala?

No — Kerala PSC LDC recruitment and rank lists are typically prepared district-wise (separate notifications and separate rank lists per district), so your district of application matters for your actual selection chances.
