Eligibility

Age RelaxationSC/ST +5 yrs · OBC +3 yrs · PwBD (UR/EWS) +10 yrs, (OBC) +13 yrs, (SC/ST) +15 yrs · Ex-servicemen +3 yrs after deducting military service (Grade D/Group C posts only) · Central Govt civilian employees with 3+ yrs service: up to 40 yrs (45 yrs SC/ST), Group C posts only · Widowed/divorced/judicially-separated women: up to 35 yrs (40 yrs SC/ST).

Exam Pattern

Two-stage selection — a single online Computer-Based Test (CBT) followed by a Skill Test in Stenography (dictation + transcription), which is qualifying in nature for Grade C/D distinctions (mode note: this is a single-tier CBT, not a Tier-1/Tier-2 CBT structure like some other SSC exams). · CBT: 2 hours, with sectional time limits (30 min + 30 min + 60 min per part, shown below).
Part I: General Intelligence & Reasoning50 questions, 50 marks (30 min)
Part II: General Awareness50 questions, 50 marks (30 min)
Part III: English Language & Comprehension100 questions, 100 marks (60 min)
Total200 questions, 200 marks
Skill Test (Stenography) — Grade C: English 100 wpm dictation, 40 min transcription; Hindi 100 wpm dictation, 55 min transcription
Skill Test (Stenography) — Grade D: English 80 wpm dictation, 50 min transcription; Hindi 80 wpm dictation, 65 min transcription
Negative Marking0.25 marks (1/4) deducted for each wrong answer in the CBT; no deduction for unattempted questions.
Passing MarksN/A — merit-based cutoff, declared after each exam, no fixed qualifying % found for the CBT. The Skill Test itself is qualifying (pass/fail on dictation-transcription accuracy) but does not carry a published fixed numeric threshold beyond meeting the speed/accuracy requirement.

Corroborated across 2+ independent secondary sources; official SSC (ssc.gov.in) notification PDF was not directly fetchable. Section-wise question/mark split (50/50/100) and skill test speed requirements were consistent across sources.

How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) on the new ssc.gov.in — the old ssc.nic.in OTR does not carry over
  2. Log in, select the Stenographer Grade C & D 2026 notice, and fill the application form
  3. Live-capture your photograph via webcam per the on-screen instructions, or opt for Aadhaar-based authentication (which relaxes the photo/ID process)
  4. Upload your scanned signature (JPEG, 10–20 KB, ~6.0 × 2.0 cm)
  5. Preview and verify every entered detail before final submission
  6. Pay the ₹100 fee online by the deadline (waived for women and SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-servicemen candidates)
  7. Use the correction window if changes are needed (correction charges apply: ₹200 first, ₹500 second)
  8. Download your Admission Certificate a few days before the exam
  9. Appear for the Computer Based Examination, then — if shortlisted — the qualifying Stenography Skill Test (dictation at 100 wpm for Grade C / 80 wpm for Grade D, then transcription on computer)
  10. Undergo Document Verification by the allotting department after the final result

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between qualifying for Grade C versus Grade D?

Grade C requires a faster stenography skill test (100 wpm dictation with less transcription time) than Grade D (80 wpm dictation, more transcription time) — your CBT score and category/preference determine which grade you're considered for, but the skill test speed requirement differs by grade.

Does my skill test performance affect my final merit rank?

The Skill Test is qualifying in nature (you must meet the minimum dictation/transcription accuracy standard), while final merit ranking is primarily determined by your CBT score — but confirm this against the specific year's notification as skill test scoring nuances can vary.

Is there a time limit for each section of the CBT, or just an overall 2-hour limit?

Each of the three sections has its own sectional time limit (30 minutes for Reasoning, 30 minutes for General Awareness, 60 minutes for English) within the overall 2-hour test — you cannot freely move time between sections.

Who is exempt from the application fee?

Women candidates, and those from SC, ST, PwBD, and Ex-Servicemen categories, are fully exempt from the Rs. 100 fee that General/other category candidates must pay.
