How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Register on the official Maharashtra Police recruitment portal (policerecruitment2025.mahait.org) with basic personal details.
  2. Fill in the online application form with educational, category and other required details.
  3. Upload scanned photograph and signature in the prescribed format.
  4. Pay the application fee online (₹450 General category; ₹350 reserved categories).
  5. Submit the form and download/print the confirmation for future reference (needed for PET/PST and written-exam admit cards).

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I take the written exam or the physical test first for Maharashtra Police Bharti?

Unlike most other state police recruitments, Maharashtra runs the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST) BEFORE the written exam — you must clear the physical round (minimum 50%) to even sit for the written test.

What language is the written exam conducted in?

Marathi — the paper is set in Marathi, so candidates should be comfortable reading and answering in that language.

Is there negative marking in the Maharashtra Police Constable written exam?

This was not confirmed in available sources — treat it as unknown and check the specific recruitment notification (policerecruitment2025.mahait.org) rather than assuming either way.

How are PET marks distributed?

Out of 50 total marks: running (1600m for men/800m for women) is worth 20 marks, the 100m sprint is worth 15 marks, and shot put is worth 15 marks — faster times and longer throws score progressively higher within each band.
