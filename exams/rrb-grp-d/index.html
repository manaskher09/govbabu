Exam Pattern

Online CBT (single stage, Level-1 posts), followed by Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Document Verification/Medical. · CBT: 90 minutes (120 minutes for PwBD candidates).
CBT100 questions/100 marks total — Mathematics, General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Science, General Awareness & Current Affairs (approx. 25 questions each, exact split varies by notification)
PETQualifying only — Male: carry 35kg for 100m in 2 min + run 1000m in 4 min 15 sec; Female: carry 20kg for 100m in 2 min + run 1000m in 5 min 40 sec (one attempt each)
Negative Marking-1/3 mark per wrong answer in CBT.
Passing MarksQualifying percentage in CBT: UR/EWS 40%, OBC(NCL)/SC 30%, ST 30%. (PET is qualifying only, pass/fail, no marks.)

Fee, CBT pattern (100Q/100 marks/90 min, -1/3 negative marking) and qualifying percentages agree multiple coaching-site sources — secondary-sourced, moderate-high confidence. The exact section-wise question split (25/25/25/25) is commonly cited but not confirmed against an official PDF for this cycle.

How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Create an account on rrbapply.gov.in (Aadhaar/DigiLocker verification recommended)
  2. Fill the application form
  3. Live-capture your photo during the application — there is no separate upload
  4. Upload your signature to spec
  5. Pay the fee — ₹500 for UR/OBC-NCL/EWS (₹400 refunded after appearing in CBT) or ₹250 for SC/ST/women/PwBD/Ex-servicemen/Transgender/Minorities/EBC (fully refunded after appearing in CBT); refund credited only to an Aadhaar-seeded bank account

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Group D really just one written exam, or is there a separate physical test round like Group D used to have?

Selection is CBT followed by a qualifying Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Document Verification/Medical — there's no separate second written stage like NTPC's CBT-2.

If I fail the PET, can I retake it?

No, each PET event (carrying weight, running) is a single-attempt qualifying test — there's no retry within the same cycle.

Do women have easier PET norms than men?

Yes, the weight-carry and running distance/time norms are relaxed for female candidates compared to male candidates.

Is the CBT fee refund automatic?

No — RRB processes refunds separately after the exam and typically requires your bank account to be Aadhaar-seeded; check the RRB refund portal/link rather than assuming it arrives automatically.
