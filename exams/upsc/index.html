ⓘ Data note

UPSC has fully retired the old One-Time Registration (OTR) system in favour of the Aadhaar-based Universal Registration Number (URN) portal at upsconline.nic.in — OTR terminology is no longer used in the candidate-facing UI as of 2026 (re-confirmed 27 Aug 2026 across multiple current sources). Signature pixel dimensions remain genuinely unresolved between sources — the 20–100 KB file-size figure is solid, the exact width/height is not (sources disagree between ~350–500 px and other pixel/DPI conversions).

Posts & Pay

Level 10 (Junior Time Scale) · ₹56,100 – ₹1,77,500 (basic)~24 Group A/B services — IAS, IPS, IFS and other Central Civil Services — allocated by rank, category and preference after the interview stage

All Group A services (including IAS/IPS) enter at the same Level 10 basic pay — DA/HRA and other allowances add to this and vary by posting.

Eligibility

Age21–32 years (general/EWS) as on 1 August of the exam year
Age RelaxationOBC (non-creamy layer) up to 35 · SC/ST up to 37 · PwBD up to 42
QualificationBachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university (final-year candidates may apply provisionally in some cycles).

Career Progression

Not an official document — a commonly cited coaching-site estimate for the IAS career path.

  • Sub-Divisional Magistrate — first 0–4 years
  • Additional Collector — around 4–9 years
  • District Magistrate / Collector — around 9–13 years
  • Joint Secretary, Government of India — around 17–22 years
  • Secretary, Government of India — around 30–35 years
  • Actual timelines vary a lot by state cadre and how many officers joined in your batch year

How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Register on the current UPSC application portal (upsconline.nic.in) via the Universal Registration Number (URN) system — the old OTR system has been fully retired
  2. Fill the common application form, then the exam-specific Civil Services application
  3. Upload photo and signature to spec
  4. Pay the fee online (exemptions apply for SC/ST/PwBD/women per UPSC norms)
  5. Submit before the deadline — corrections are typically allowed only in a short post-close window
