📝 How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Register on the current UPSC application portal (upsconline.nic.in) via the Universal Registration Number (URN) system — the old OTR system has been fully retired
  2. Fill the common application form, then the exam-specific Civil Services application
  3. Upload photo and signature to spec
  4. Pay the fee online (exemptions apply for SC/ST/PwBD/women per UPSC norms)
  5. Submit before the deadline — corrections are typically allowed only in a short post-close window

🧭 Selection Process

Offline OMR-based objective (pen-and-paper), NOT computer-based — Prelims is two objective papers on one day. (Mains is a separate, later, descriptive/written stage;Interview follows Mains.This entry covers Prelims, the first screening stage.)

📋 Exam Pattern

2 hours per paper (Paper I and Paper II), held in two sessions on the same day = 4 hours total exam day. · Negative Marking: 1/3 mark deducted per wrong answer on both papers (no deduction for unattempted questions). Reported per-question deductions of "0.66" (GS-I, 200/100Q≈2 marks/Q, 1/3 of 2 = 0.66) and "0.83" (CSAT, 200/80Q=2.5 marks/Q, 1/3 of 2.5=0.83) in secondary sources are consistent with the standard 1/3-mark rule, just expressed as absolute marks lost per question rather than a fraction. · Passing Marks: CSAT (GS Paper II) has a fixed qualifying threshold of 33% (66/200 marks) — candidates must clear this to have GS Paper I evaluated for the Prelims merit list. GS Paper I itself has no fixed passing %; the Prelims cutoff is decided each year based on vacancies/normalization-free raw scores (UPSC does not normalize Prelims across sessions since there is only one shift).

SectionDetails
General Studies Paper I (GS)100 questions, 200 marks — history, geography, polity, economy, environment, science & tech, current affairs; counts toward the Prelims cutoff/merit
General Studies Paper II (CSAT)80 questions, 200 marks — comprehension, reasoning, analytical ability, basic numeracy/data interpretation; qualifying only, does not count toward ranking

Secondary-sourced from multiple coaching-site aggregators, which agree on paper structure and the 33% CSAT qualifying mark; official UPSC PDF at upsc.gov.in was not directly fetched (site typically blocks automated fetches).The 1/3-mark negative marking rule and 200-mark-per-paper structure are long-standing and well-corroborated across all sources.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does a low CSAT score hurt my UPSC Prelims result?

No, as long as you clear the 33% (66/200) qualifying threshold in CSAT — after that, only your GS Paper I score is used to decide who moves to Mains, so CSAT beyond the cutoff doesn't add to your rank.

If I don't attempt a question, do I lose marks?

No — the 1/3 negative marking only applies to questions you attempt and answer incorrectly. Leaving a question blank costs you nothing.

Is the UPSC Prelims exam computer-based like SSC/IBPS exams?

No — unlike most SSC and banking exams, UPSC Prelims (and Mains) are conducted offline using OMR sheets (Prelims) and handwritten answer books (Mains), not on a computer.

Do women or SC/ST/PwBD candidates get an application fee waiver?

Yes — the Rs 100 fee is fully waived for women (all categories) and for SC, ST, and PwBD candidates; only General/EWS/OBC male candidates pay the fee.
