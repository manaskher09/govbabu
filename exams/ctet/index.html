Posts & Pay

Paper I (qualifies for Classes 1–5, Primary Teacher) · ~7th CPC Level 6, from ₹35,400 basic — coaching-site estimate, not verified against an official pay-matrix notificationPrimary Teacher (PRT) posts in central government schools (KVS/NVS) and many state recruitment exams that accept CTET
Paper II (qualifies for Classes 6–8, Trained Graduate Teacher) · ~7th CPC Level 7, from ₹44,900 basic — same caveat as aboveTrained Graduate Teacher (TGT) posts, same recruiting bodies

CTET is a certification, not a recruitment exam — passing it doesn't itself pay a salary. It's a lifetime-valid eligibility certificate required before you can apply to actual teacher-recruitment exams (KVS, NVS, Central Tibetan Schools Administration, and most state TET-equivalent recruitment).

Eligibility

AgeMinimum 18 years; no upper age limit
QualificationPaper I: 12th pass (≥50%) + 2-yr Diploma in Elementary Education, or 12th (≥50%) + 4-yr B.El.Ed, or an equivalent NCTE-recognized qualification. Paper II: Graduation (≥50%) + B.Ed, or 12th (≥50%) + 4-yr B.El.Ed/B.A.Ed/B.Sc.Ed, or graduation (≥45%) + B.Ed per NCTE norms. These clauses have several state-specific sub-variants (e.g. BTC, JBT) — confirm the exact wording against the official Information Bulletin for your qualification path.

Exam Pattern

Offline, OMR-based (pen-and-paper) · 2 hours 30 minutes per paper
Paper I (Classes 1–5)150 MCQs / 150 marks — Child Development & Pedagogy (30), Language I (30), Language II (30), Mathematics (30), Environmental Studies (30)
Paper II (Classes 6–8)150 MCQs / 150 marks — Child Development & Pedagogy (30), Language I (30), Language II (30), then EITHER Mathematics & Science (60, for Maths/Science teachers) OR Social Studies (60, for SST teachers)
Negative MarkingNone, per secondary reporting — not independently confirmed against a primary CBSE document this pass
Passing Marks60% for General category, 55% for SC/ST/OBC/PwD — a qualifying cutoff, not a competitive rank, since CTET has no fixed number of seats

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CTET itself a government job?

No — CTET only certifies that you're eligible to be considered for a teaching post. Clearing it doesn't hire you or pay a salary; you still have to apply separately to KVS, NVS, DSSSB or a state recruitment exam that accepts CTET.

How long does a CTET certificate stay valid?

For life, per CBSE's current rule (secondary-sourced — this has changed before, so it's worth confirming against your own certificate/the current Information Bulletin rather than assuming it'll never change again).

Do I need to clear both Paper I and Paper II?

No. Take Paper I alone if you only want to teach Classes 1–5, Paper II alone for Classes 6–8, or both (each has its own fee) if you want to keep both age groups open.

Is there negative marking?

No, per secondary reporting — every question is multiple-choice with no penalty for a wrong answer. Not independently confirmed against a primary CBSE document this pass, so double-check your admit card / Information Bulletin.
