📝 How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Register at ibps.in under the CRP CSA link, choosing one state/UT to apply for
  2. Fill the form and upload photo, signature, left thumb impression and handwritten declaration
  3. Pay the fee — ₹175 (SC/ST/PwBD/ESM), ₹850 (others)
  4. Use the 2-day post-close edit window if needed (₹200 correction fee)
  5. Download the call letter from ibps.in when released

Not stated in the official notification — a commonly cited coaching-site estimate.

🧭 Selection Process

Online CBT (Prelims + Mains, both fully objective — no descriptive component); final stage is document verification (no interview for Clerk)

📋 Exam Pattern

Prelims: 60 minutes | Mains: 125 minutes · Negative Marking: -0.25 (1/4 mark) per wrong answer in both Prelims and Mains; no penalty for unattempted questions · Passing Marks: N/A — merit-based ranking with sectional and overall cutoffs that vary by year; no fixed qualifying percentage published

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does IBPS Clerk have an interview stage?

No — unlike IBPS PO, selection for IBPS Clerk is based on the Prelims + Mains written exams only, with no personal interview; final merit is based solely on Mains marks.

Are Prelims marks added to the Mains score for the final result?

No, Prelims marks are used only to shortlist candidates for Mains; the final provisional allotment is based entirely on Mains marks.

Is there a separate Computer Knowledge section in Mains?

No standalone Computer Knowledge paper — computer-based questions are merged into the "Reasoning Ability & Computer Aptitude" section.

What is the sectional time limit rule in the Clerk exam?

Each section in both Prelims and Mains has its own fixed timer; you cannot use extra time saved in one section for another.
