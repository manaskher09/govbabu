ⓘ Data note

Kerala PSC does not run this as one exam with a single vacancy total, pay scale or qualification line the way BPSC/UPPSC/OPSC do — it is a shared OMR screening test (2 May 2026) feeding 21 separately-notified degree-level category/post recruitments, each carrying its own vacancy count, pay scale and exact qualification, published in that category's own notification. Reported scale for context only (not vacancies): 26.77 lakh applications, 5.55 lakh confirmed participants for this cycle. Confirmed officially on keralapsc.gov.in: no application fee is charged for Kerala PSC exams (candidates use a free One-Time Registration profile instead), and the age-reckoning date is 1 January of the notification year, with SC/ST getting 5 years' relaxation and OBC 3 years. The original application-opening date, total vacancy count, EWS/PwBD relaxation figures, exact pixel/KB photo-signature specs, and the precise selection-stage marks breakdown were not found in this research pass and should not be assumed — check the specific category notification you're applying under.

Exam Pattern

The "Degree Level Prelims" itself is a single common OMR-based objective screening test shared across multiple degree-level posts. IMPORTANT CAVEAT: what happens AFTER prelims varies by post — most ordinary degree-level posts go straight from this common prelims to a post-specific objective Main exam (no interview), while a small number of higher/administrative posts (e.g., Kerala Administrative Service) layer on descriptive Mains papers plus an interview. Sources found conflate these two tracks, so the "Mains + Interview" details below should NOT be assumed to apply to every degree-level post using this common prelims — verify per-post on keralapsc.gov.in. · Prelims: 1 hour 15 minutes. How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) once at keralapsc.gov.in — free of cost
  2. Log in and apply to the specific notified category/post you're eligible for, before that category's own deadline
  3. For this shared Degree Level Prelims specifically, complete the separate candidate confirmation step during the designated window (2026: 27 Feb – 18 Mar) — without it, no hall ticket is generated
  4. Download your hall ticket closer to the exam date
  5. Appear for the shared Preliminary exam, then proceed through your specific category's own Main Examination and further stages (interview/skill test/document verification) where applicable

Frequently Asked Questions

If I clear the Kerala PSC Degree Level Prelims, am I guaranteed a Mains exam and interview?

Not necessarily — the common Degree Level Prelims is shared as a screening test across many degree-level posts, but what comes after it depends on the specific post. Most ordinary posts move to a post-specific objective Main exam with no interview; only certain higher administrative posts (like KAS) add descriptive Mains papers plus an interview. Check your specific post's notification.

Do my Prelims marks count toward my final selection?

No — the Degree Level Prelims is purely a qualifying/screening stage. Only your subsequent post-specific Main exam (and Interview, if your post has one) marks count toward final selection.

Is there negative marking in the Degree Level Prelims?

Yes — 0.33 marks are deducted for every wrong answer, with no penalty for questions left unattempted.

Can one Degree Level Prelims score be used for multiple posts/notifications?

This depends on Kerala PSC's specific "common preliminary examination" scheme rules for the cycle — the mechanism is designed to let one prelims score feed shortlisting for multiple degree-level posts notified around the same time, but the exact posts covered vary per notification, so check the specific notification's list of "included posts" rather than assuming universal coverage.
