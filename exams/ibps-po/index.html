📝 How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Register at ibps.in under the CRP PO/MT link
  2. Fill the application form and choose participating-bank/centre preferences
  3. Upload photo, signature, left thumb impression, handwritten declaration and 10th certificate
  4. Pay the fee online — ₹175 (SC/ST/PwBD), ₹850 (others)
  5. Use the 2-day post-close edit window if needed (₹200 correction fee)
  6. Download the call letter from ibps.in when released — no postal copies are sent

🧭 Selection Process

Online CBT (Prelims + Mains objective), plus a typed Descriptive Test in Mains; final stage is Interview

Prelims: 1 hour (60 min) | Mains: 190 minutes total (160 min objective + 30 min descriptive) · Negative Marking: -0.25 (1/4 mark) per wrong answer in Prelims and Mains objective tests; no penalty for unattempted questions; no negative marking on the Descriptive Test · Passing Marks: N/A — merit-based ranking with sectional and overall cutoffs that vary by year; no fixed qualifying percentage published

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do Prelims marks count toward the final IBPS PO merit list?

No. Prelims is only a qualifying/shortlisting stage — only Mains (objective + descriptive) marks, combined with interview marks, decide the final merit list.

Is there a sectional cutoff in IBPS PO Prelims?

Yes, candidates must clear both the sectional cutoff in each of the three Prelims sections and the overall cutoff to be shortlisted for Mains.

What happens if I skip the Descriptive Test in Mains?

The Descriptive Test (Essay & Comprehension) is qualifying in nature but mandatory — skipping it or scoring below the qualifying threshold can disqualify you even if your objective score is strong.

Has Computer Aptitude been removed as a separate Mains section?

Yes, per the 2026 revised pattern, Computer Aptitude is no longer a standalone Mains section; Data Analysis & Interpretation and General/Economy/Banking/Digital Awareness are the current sections alongside Reasoning and English.
