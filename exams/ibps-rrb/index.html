📝 How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Register at ibps.in under the CRP RRBs XIV link, choosing the Officer or Office Assistant application separately as applicable
  2. Fill the application form with personal, educational and category details, and select RRB/state preferences per the notification's option rules
  3. Upload photograph (or live-capture via webcam/mobile), signature, left thumb impression and handwritten declaration as per the specifications above; also upload SSC/10th-standard and category certificates where applicable
  4. Pay the fee online — ₹175 (SC/ST/PwBD, plus Ex-servicemen for Office Assistant) or ₹850 (all others), inclusive of GST
  5. Use the post-close Edit Window (announced separately on ibps.in) if corrections are needed
  6. Download call letters for the Preliminary, Main/Single exam and (for Officer posts) interview from ibps.in when released

🧭 Selection Process

Online CBT.Officer Scale-I and Office Assistant have Prelims (qualifying) + Mains;Officer Scale-II and III (specialist/generalist) have a Single Combined Written Exam only, followed by interview for Scale I/II/III (Office Assistant has no interview)

📋 Exam Pattern

Prelims (Officer Scale-I / Office Assistant): 45 minutes | Mains (both Officer Scale-I and Office Assistant): 120 minutes (2 hours) · Negative Marking: -0.25 (1/4 mark) per wrong answer in both Prelims and Mains; no penalty for unattempted questions · Passing Marks: N/A — Prelims is qualifying in nature (candidates must clear sectional and overall cutoffs to advance) but the cutoff itself is normalized and varies by year; no fixed qualifying percentage published for Mains either

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is IBPS RRB Officer Scale-I different in exam pattern from IBPS PO?

Yes — RRB Officer Scale-I Prelims has just 2 sections (Reasoning, Numerical Ability) instead of 3, is shorter (45 min vs 60 min), and RRB has no separate Descriptive Test in Mains, unlike IBPS PO.

Do Officer Scale-II and III candidates take a Prelims exam?

No — Officer Scale-II (Specialist/Generalist) and Scale-III posts have only a single online Mains-equivalent exam, no separate Prelims stage.

Is there a language option in the IBPS RRB Mains exam?

Yes, candidates can generally choose between English and Hindi for the language-based section of the exam.

Does Office Assistant recruitment include an interview?

No, Office Assistant (Multipurpose) selection is based on Prelims + Mains only, with no interview; Officer posts (Scale I/II/III) do include an interview stage.
