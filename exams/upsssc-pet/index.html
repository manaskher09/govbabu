ⓘ Data note

PET is a two-day, screening-only test — it does not itself carry vacancies or a salary; a valid PET score (3-year validity) is a precondition to apply for specific UPSSSC Group B/C posts, each with its own vacancy count and pay. This entry covers the PET 2026 cycle, which is genuinely open as of 03 Sep 2026 (application window closes 07 Sep 2026). The PET 2026 exam date has not been officially announced — the Nov–Dec 2026 estimate comes from coaching/job-alert sites, not the notification itself, which I could not fetch directly in this session (upsssc.gov.in returned a certificate/network error to automated fetch tools). Application fee (₹185 Gen/OBC, ₹95 SC/ST, ₹25 PH) and the age-relaxation table are likewise drawn from secondary aggregators summarizing the official PDF — verify against the notification on upsssc.gov.in before applying.

How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Complete UPSSSC One-Time Registration (OTR) at otr.upsssc.gov.in with mobile/email OTP verification, personal details, educational qualifications and Aadhaar details, and upload photo/signature.
  2. Once OTR is verified and an OTR number is generated, go to upsssc.gov.in and open the "PET 2026 Apply Online" link.
  3. Log in with the OTR number, check the pre-filled OTR details, and fill in any additional exam-specific fields.
  4. Upload any remaining required documents (e.g. category/domicile certificate) in the prescribed format.
  5. Pay the application fee online (Debit/Credit Card, Net Banking or UPI).
  6. Review the completed form carefully, submit it, and download/print the confirmation page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is UPSSSC PET itself a job recruitment exam?

No — PET is only a qualifying screening test. Clearing it (score valid for 3 years) makes you eligible to apply for a range of UP Group C/D posts (like UP Lekhpal, various UPSSSC recruitments), each of which has its own separate main exam and merit list.

What is the minimum score needed to qualify PET?

Secondary sources consistently report a fixed qualifying mark of 33 out of 100 (33%), regardless of category, though this should be verified against the latest official notification since it's a policy figure that could change.

How long is a PET score valid, and can I use one PET score for multiple recruitments?

A qualifying PET score is valid for 3 years and can be used to apply for multiple UPSSSC/UP state recruitments during that validity window, without needing to retake PET for each one.

Does PET have sectional negative marking or just an overall one?

Negative marking is applied per question across the whole paper (0.25 marks per wrong answer) rather than as separate sectional penalties, so a wrong guess in any section costs the same.
