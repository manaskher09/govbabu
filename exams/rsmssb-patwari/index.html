This is the most recently completed Patwari cycle. RSMSSB (Rajasthan Staff Selection Board, also styled RSSB, both rsmssb.rajasthan.gov.in and rssb.rajasthan.gov.in are used in official communication) reopened the notification in mid-2025 and revised the vacancy count upward from 2,020 to 3,705 before the application window closed on 29 Jun 2025. How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Complete Rajasthan SSO ID registration (One-Time Registration) if not already done, providing basic personal details.
  2. Log in to rsmssb.rajasthan.gov.in (or rssb.rajasthan.gov.in) with your SSO ID and password.
  3. Go to the Recruitment section and select the Patwari Recruitment 2025 link.
  4. Read the official notification carefully, then click Apply Online and fill in the application form.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature and required educational/category certificates in the prescribed format.
  6. Pay the application fee online (₹800 General; ₹400 for Rajasthan OBC/EBC/EWS/SC/ST of the non-creamy layer).
  7. Submit the form and save/print the confirmation for future reference.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many marks is each question worth, and how much do I lose for a wrong answer?

Each question carries 2 marks (150 questions for 300 total marks), with 1/3rd of a mark deducted for every wrong answer — so guessing carries real risk compared to leaving a question blank.

Why does this exam have 5 answer options instead of the usual 4?

Secondary sources report RSMSSB Patwari uses 5 options (A-E) per question rather than the standard 4, which is a distinguishing feature of this exam's OMR sheet — verify this detail against your specific admit card/notification.

Is there a separate skill test or interview after the written exam?

Based on available sources, Patwari selection is primarily through the written exam and document verification; no separate interview or typing/skill test was reported for this post.

Do reserved-category candidates get a fee concession?

Yes — OBC/EBC/EWS/SC/ST (non-creamy-layer, Rajasthan-domicile) and PwD candidates pay Rs. 400 versus Rs. 800 for General category candidates.
