Posts & Pay

Level 1 · ₹18,000 – ₹56,900 (basic, 7th CPC Pay Matrix)Multi-Tasking Staff (Non-Technical) — Group C, non-gazetted, non-ministerial posts across central government Ministries/Departments/Offices and Constitutional/Statutory Bodies, in various States/UTs
Level 1 · ₹18,000 – ₹56,900 (basic; same Pay Level as MTS)Havaldar — Central Board of Indirect Taxes & Customs (CBIC) and Central Bureau of Narcotics (CBN), Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance. Recruited through the SAME notification/exam as MTS, but Havaldar candidates additionally must clear a Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST) that MTS candidates do not.

Confirmed from the official Notice, Para 1: MTS and Havaldar are both Pay Level-1 posts despite the different departments/duties. In-hand pay adds Dearness Allowance, HRA and Transport Allowance depending on posting.

Eligibility

Age
  • Most MTS posts: 18–25 years, as on 01 Aug 2025 (born between 02 Aug 2000 and 01 Aug 2007)
  • Havaldar (CBIC/CBN) and a few specified MTS posts: 18–27 years (born not before 02 Aug 1998)
  • Exact age band depends on your specific post — check the vacancy annexure
Age RelaxationSC/ST +5 years · OBC +3 years · PwBD (Unreserved/EWS) +10 years · PwBD (OBC) +13 years · PwBD (SC/ST) +15 years · Ex-servicemen +3 years after deducting military service · defence personnel disabled in action +3 years (+8 for SC/ST) · Central Govt civilian employees with 3+ years' service up to age 40 (45 for SC/ST) · widows/divorced/judicially-separated women up to age 35 (40 for SC/ST).
QualificationMatriculation / Class 10 pass from a recognized Board. Havaldar candidates additionally must clear a Physical Efficiency Test (walking 1,600 m in 15 min male / 1 km in 20 min female) and Physical Standard Test (height 157.5 cm male, relaxable to 152.5 cm for Garhwali/Assamese/Gorkha/ST candidates — chest 81 cm expanded with 5 cm minimum expansion; height 152 cm female, weight 48 kg, both relaxable for the same categories). MTS itself carries no PET/PST requirement.

Exam Pattern

Computer-Based Examination (CBE/online), single paper split into two mandatory sessions on the same sitting; PET/PST additionally required only for the Havaldar (CBIC/CBN) post. · 45 minutes per session (two sessions = 90 minutes total; scribe-eligible candidates get 60 minutes per session).
Session I: Numerical & Mathematical Ability + Reasoning & Problem-Solving40 questions, 120 marks, no negative marking
Session II: General Awareness + English Language50 questions (25 each), 150 marks, negative marking applies
Negative MarkingNo negative marking in Session I. In Session II, -1 mark per wrong answer.
Passing MarksN/A — merit-based ranking, no fixed qualifying %. Both sessions are compulsory; skipping either session leads to disqualification regardless of score.

Secondary-sourced from multiple coaching-site aggregators, which agree on the 40+50 question split, 120+150 marks, and session-wise negative marking rule; official SSC MTS notification PDF not directly fetched. Fee figure (Rs 100, standard exemptions) confirmed by 2 independent sources but PwBD/ESM exemption specifics for MTS specifically were not triple-verified — treat as a reasonable inference from standard SSC fee policy, not a hard confirmation.

How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) on the new ssc.gov.in portal (an old ssc.nic.in OTR will not carry over) or via the official "mySSC" mobile app
  2. Opt in to Aadhaar-Based Authentication if possible, to avoid strict photo/signature rejection rules and carrying printed ID to the exam
  3. Log in and fill the MTS/Havaldar application — 10th-pass qualification, your age-group (18–25 or 18–27), and whether you wish to be considered for Havaldar (CBIC/CBN)
  4. Give your Post-cum-State/UT/Cadre-Controlling-Authority preferences in strict priority order using the codes in the Notice's annexure — this cannot be changed later
  5. Sit for the live in-application webcam photo capture when prompted (no photo upload option exists)
  6. Upload your scanned signature (6.0 cm × 2.0 cm, JPEG/JPG, 10–20 KB)
  7. Pay the ₹100 fee online — waived for women, SC, ST, PwBD and Ex-servicemen — by the fee deadline
  8. Use the 3-day correction window right after closing to fix errors (₹200 first correction, ₹500 second)
  9. Download your Admission Certificate and sit the two-session Computer Based Examination (Session-I: Numerical & Reasoning; Session-II: General Awareness & English — negative marking applies only in Session-II)
  10. If shortlisted for Havaldar, additionally appear for the Physical Efficiency Test / Physical Standard Test at a CBIC/CBN-notified centre

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is there no negative marking in Session I but there is in Session II?

SSC's current MTS pattern only penalizes wrong answers in Session II (General Awareness + English); Session I (Numerical Ability + Reasoning) is scored purely on correct answers with no penalty for wrong attempts, so guessing is safer there.

What's different about the Havaldar post compared to regular MTS posts?

Havaldar (in CBIC/Central Board of Narcotics) candidates go through the same written exam but additionally must clear a Physical Efficiency Test/Physical Standard Test — a requirement that doesn't apply to general MTS posts.

Can I skip Session I if I only care about my Session II score?

No — both sessions are compulsory in the same sitting; not attempting either session disqualifies you regardless of how well you do in the other.

Is SSC MTS available in regional languages?

Yes — Session I and the General Awareness portion of Session II are offered in English, Hindi, and 13 regional languages, though the English Language section itself is tested in English.
