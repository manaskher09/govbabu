📈 Career Path

No promotion timeline is stated by SSC — JE is an entry-level post.

  • Career progression (for example, to Assistant Engineer) is decided independently by each recruiting department's own rules
  • Specific "X years to promotion" figures seen on coaching sites are unverified — don't treat them as official

🧭 Selection Process

Computer-Based Test (CBT/online) for both papers.Paper I is objective;Paper II is also CBT (conventional/descriptive engineering paper delivered on computer in some cycles — sources describe it as objective with per-question marks, so treated as objective here).

📋 Exam Pattern

Paper I: 2 hours (2 hours 40 minutes for scribe-eligible candidates). Paper II: 2 hours. · Negative Marking: -0.25 per wrong answer in Paper I; -1 mark per wrong answer in Paper II. · Passing Marks: N/A — merit-based ranking, no fixed qualifying %. Paper I is used to shortlist for Paper II; final merit is based on Paper II score (plus Paper I for those it counts toward, per SSC's combined scoring rules, which vary slightly by cycle).

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Paper II have much heavier negative marking (-1) than Paper I (-0.25)?

Paper II is the specialized, higher-stakes engineering paper that primarily decides final merit, so SSC penalizes wrong answers more heavily there to discourage guessing on technical questions.

Do all engineering streams (Civil, Electrical, Mechanical) take the same Paper I?

The General Intelligence and General Awareness portions of Paper I are common to all streams, but the General Engineering portion within Paper I (and all of Paper II) is specific to the stream you apply under.

How many vacancies does the 2026 SSC JE cycle have, and when does the application window close?

Per recent coverage, the 2026 notification lists 1,748 tentative Junior Engineer vacancies, with the application window opening around 2 September 2026 and closing around 22-23 September 2026 — confirm exact dates on ssc.gov.in since these can shift.

Is there a document verification stage after the written exams?

Yes — after Paper I and Paper II, shortlisted candidates typically go through document verification (and in some streams, certificate/qualification checks) before final selection; this is a standard SSC JE stage though not a "marks" component.
