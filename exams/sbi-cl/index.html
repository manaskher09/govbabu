❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does SBI Clerk have an interview round?

No, SBI Clerk (Junior Associate) selection is purely on the basis of Prelims + Mains written exams; there is no interview.

What is the Local Language Proficiency Test?

A qualifying-only test (not scored into merit) that candidates who don't already have proof of studying the local language in school/college must clear after Mains, before final selection.

Do Prelims marks count in the final SBI Clerk merit list?

No, only Mains marks decide the final merit list and allotment; Prelims is used only to shortlist candidates for Mains.

Is there a separate Computer Knowledge section in Mains?

No standalone section — computer-related questions are combined into the "Reasoning Ability & Computer Aptitude" section.
