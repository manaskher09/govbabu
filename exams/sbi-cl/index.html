How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Register at sbi.bank.in under Current Openings → the relevant advertisement number
  2. Choose one state/UT and fill the online form
  3. Upload photo, signature and other required documents
  4. Pay the fee — ₹750 (General/OBC/EWS), free for SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-servicemen
