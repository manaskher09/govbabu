GDS is not a regular Central Government post — engagement is governed by the separate GDS (Conduct & Engagement) Rules, 2020, pay is a Time Related Continuity Allowance (TRCA, not a standard pay-matrix salary), and there is no written exam — selection is purely by a merit list built from Class 10 marks. Registration/application window is genuinely open as of 03 Sep 2026 (closes 21 Sep 2026).

Posts & Pay

BPM (Branch Postmaster) · TRCA ₹12,000 – ₹29,380/- (initial slab, rises 3% annually subject to conditions)Branch Postmaster, single/multi-handed Branch Post Offices
ABPM / Dak Sevak · TRCA ₹10,000 – ₹24,470/- (initial slab, rises 3% annually subject to conditions)Assistant Branch Postmaster and Dak Sevak, across Post/Sorting/Mail/Delivery offices

GDS are not regular Central Government employees — TRCA plus Dearness Allowance on TRCA are their main emoluments, along with GDS Gratuity and a Service Discharge Benefit Scheme (akin to NPS). A GDS is expected to have another source of income; BPMs must arrange their own premises for the Branch Post Office at their own cost.

Eligibility

Age18–40 years as on the last date of application submission (21 Sep 2026)
Age RelaxationSC/ST +5 yrs · OBC +3 yrs · PwBD +10 yrs · PwBD+OBC +13 yrs · PwBD+SC/ST +15 yrs · EWS — no relaxation
QualificationPass in Secondary School Examination (10th standard) with passing marks in Mathematics and English from a recognised Board, and study of the locally prescribed language up to 10th standard (per Annexure-III); basic computer knowledge and cycling ability are also required.

Exam Pattern

NO WRITTEN EXAM. Selection is entirely merit-list-based on Class 10th (Matriculation/SSLC) board exam aggregate marks — there is no CBT, OMR test, descriptive paper, or interview of any kind. India Post prepares circle-wise and category-wise automatic merit lists purely by 10th-standard marks percentage, followed by document verification of shortlisted candidates.
Negative MarkingN/A — no written exam, merit list only.
Passing MarksN/A — no written exam, merit list only. (Category-wise and circle-wise numerical cutoff percentages of Class 10 marks are declared after each recruitment cycle/merit list, similar to a merit-based cutoff, but there is no exam to "pass" or "fail.")

This is well-corroborated across multiple sources (English editions and other secondary sources) — India Post GDS recruitment has used the 10th-marks-only merit system for years, with multiple merit lists released per cycle (e.g., 6 merit lists were released through 2025 for one recruitment cycle) and ties broken by candidate age (older preferred).

How to Apply

Apply on the official site ↗
  1. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) on indiapost.gov.in/gdsonlineengagement (31 Aug – 19 Sep 2026); prior schedules' registrations are not valid — register afresh.
  2. Log in and fill the online application, selecting Circle/Division and post preferences (02–21 Sep 2026).
  3. Upload photograph (320×400px, 30–100KB JPEG) and signature (300×120px, 20–100KB JPEG) per the prescribed specs.
  4. Enter your Class 10 marksheet subject-wise marks/grades accurately — the merit list is generated purely from this data (no written exam).
  5. Pay the ₹100 application fee via net banking/UPI/card if applicable (female, SC/ST, PwBD and transwomen applicants are exempted).
  6. Use the edit/correction window (23–24 Sep 2026) to fix any registration or application errors before final submission closes.
  7. Await the system-generated merit list based on aggregated 10th-standard percentage; shortlisted candidates undergo document verification.

Document Requirements

Photo320 × 400 px (recommended, 4:5 aspect ratio) · 30–100 KB · JPG/JPEG — plain white or light-coloured background, face occupying 70–80% of frame, no shadows/red-eye; DPI 72–150.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to take any test or interview for GDS recruitment?

No — there is no written exam, computer test, or interview at all. Selection is based purely on your Class 10th board exam marks percentage, ranked circle-wise and category-wise.

How is the merit list calculated if my 10th-grade board used a different marking scheme (grades vs percentages)?

India Post has a standard formula to convert CGPA/grades to an equivalent percentage for boards that don't award raw percentages — check the specific year's notification for the exact conversion table, since this detail affects your calculated merit score directly.

What happens after I appear in a merit list?

You must complete document verification at your allotted post office/postal division (typically within about 15 days of the list being published); provisional selection becomes final only after successful verification of your original certificates.

If I don't feature in the first merit list, is my application rejected?

Not necessarily — India Post releases multiple sequential merit lists per recruitment cycle (to fill vacancies left by non-joiners or rejected candidates), so candidates already in the applicant pool can appear in later lists too.
