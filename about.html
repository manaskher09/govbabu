👋 About

Why GovtBabu exists

Application forms for Indian exams and jobs are strict about file sizes and formats — GovtBabu is a small, focused tool to get a photo, signature or scan into shape in seconds, without handing your documents to a server.

🔒 Privacy by default

Every tool runs client-side. Your photo, signature and PDF files are processed in your browser's memory and never leave your device.

🎯 Built for real forms

Target sizes are shaped around how Indian exam and job application portals actually ask for files — exact KB limits, specific formats, per exam.

🧭 Honest about gaps

Where we haven't verified an exam's exact spec against its latest official notification yet, we say so — no guessing on your behalf.
