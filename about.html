👋 About GovtBabu

More than a document tool

GovtBabu started as a way to resize a photo and signature to the exact spec an exam demands. It's grown into a single place to understand a government exam properly — how many seats there are, who's eligible, how reservation and age relaxation actually apply to you, exactly how to apply — and then get your documents ready. All researched and sourced, not guessed.

What you get, in one place

🔍 Know the exam

Seats, pay levels, eligibility, age relaxation and reservation policy — researched from the official notification for every exam we cover, sources cited and gaps flagged honestly rather than guessed.

🧭 Know how to apply

Step-by-step instructions through the actual application portal — what to expect at each stage, and how correction windows work if you make a mistake.

🖼️ Get your documents ready

Your photo, signature and other required documents, resized to the exact pixel and KB spec your exam demands — processed entirely in your browser.

📌 Stay on top of it

A notice board for every exam currently accepting applications, a calendar sorted by deadline, and a results section that links straight to each exam's official result page.

🔒 Privacy by default

Every document tool runs client-side. Your photo, signature and PDF files are processed in your browser's memory and never leave your device.

📚 Sourced, not guessed

Every seat count, pay level, eligibility rule and deadline is researched against the official notification wherever one exists. Where sources conflict or something can't be confirmed, we say so instead of picking one silently.

🎉 Free for aspirants

GovtBabu is free for every aspirant to use right now — no login, no payment — for at least the first two months.

Roadmap

What's next

  • Currently free for everyone — no payment is required right now while we gather feedback. A small one-time fee may return later, and only for the document-prep tools — never for exam information.
  • Growing exam coverage — actively adding more high-volume exams (Railway, SSC, Police, Defence, Banking and more) beyond the original list, each researched the same way.
  • Keep exam data current — application windows open and close constantly, so dates, seats and statuses need periodic re-checking, not a one-time fix.
